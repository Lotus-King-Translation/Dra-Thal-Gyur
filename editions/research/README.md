# Research provenance and cautions

[Original witness worksheet](webuddhist-witness-worksheet.xlsx): XLSX export of [the shared WeBuddhist/OpenPecha sheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1q1dNSNkrsSAWh5Ihty-teSlb3Ti3jfk77IOhE1sZdV8). The export is retained unchanged. It is not a new editorial judgement about every witness.

Original supplied TXT files now live with their editions: [Adzom](../adzom-2000/a.txt), [Sichuan](../sichuan-2016/s.txt), and [Tharpaling](../tharpaling-1983/b.txt). Redundant copies were removed only after byte equality was verified. [original-text-files.json](original-text-files.json) records canonical Drive links, byte counts, hashes, and the full-text comparison against the DOCX extractions. The originals include a UTF-8 signature; comparison decodes that signature and strips outer whitespace only. The original bytes are unchanged.

The additional report supplied by the project owner was used as an acquisition lead, not an authority over the scans. Its recommendation to use Sichuan does not replace the owner's selection of Adzom. Its external links had not been tested in that report. The present catalogue and access metadata distinguish what was actually obtained.

Matching openings do not establish a single recension. Differences in e-texts are not confirmed variants of the underlying print until checked against the scan. Provider-cleaned status is not independent proofreading certification. The report's statement that the commentary could not be verified is superseded for this project by the separately assembled The-Great-Commentary repository.
