# Research inputs and acquisition qualifications

[webuddhist-witness-worksheet.xlsx](webuddhist-witness-worksheet.xlsx) is an unedited export of the [shared WeBuddhist/OpenPecha worksheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1q1dNSNkrsSAWh5Ihty-teSlb3Ti3jfk77IOhE1sZdV8). It contains 11 witness rows and marks cleaned text finished for Adzom W1KG11703, Tharpaling W27491, and Sichuan W3CN7084. Those three original DOCX files are stored in their respective edition folders. The source worksheet was not edited.

The worksheet's status is provider documentation, not a claim that this project has proofread the e-texts. The plain-text derivatives were extracted without Tibetan corrections by [extract_etexts.py](../extract_etexts.py).

## Differences between the report and this acquisition

Adzom remains the active base because that was the explicit project choice. The report's preference for Sichuan is not adopted as a new source-selection decision.

Internet Archive currently marks W3CN7084 as copyright/access-restricted. We obtained its shared cleaned e-text, not a complete openly downloadable scan. The earlier report explicitly said that its external links had not been tested.

The worksheet links a different CTRC part; the separately verified root record is MW3CN3207_O3CN3207_NAKR4R. The original worksheet is preserved rather than silently corrected.

The ten downloaded image sets include two with gaps in the provider manifest. Their omitted indices are listed explicitly in the edition image manifests and READMEs. Complete download of the available images does not prove complete textual transmission or foliation.

No recension or stemmatic relationship is established by this acquisition. Shared opening lines alone do not establish a single recension. No new OCR, translation, emendation, or collation was performed.
