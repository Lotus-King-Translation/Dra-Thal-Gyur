## Coverage limitations

This is an acquisition snapshot, not an assertion that every extant witness has been found. Scan sets with missing numerical indices are explicitly identified above. A manifest gap is not by itself proof of missing textual content; opening and closing pages alone do not prove intervening completeness. A page-by-page foliation and collation audit remains necessary. Catalogue-only and restricted items must never be counted as files acquired.
