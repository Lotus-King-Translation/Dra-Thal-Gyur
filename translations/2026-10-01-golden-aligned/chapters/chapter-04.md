## Chapter 4

<a id="u04306"></a>

Then the Lord of Gods, Maker of Joy,

<a id="u04307"></a>

asked the teacher, the great all-pervading lord,

<a id="u04308"></a>

about the intrinsic nature of the Array of the Nature of Phenomena.

<a id="u04309"></a>

‘Kye kye! Truly complete buddha,

<a id="u04310"></a>

through the Array of Primordial Knowing,

<a id="u04311"></a>

I have gained respite from the activities of samsara,

<a id="u04312"></a>

and been uplifted on the path of passing beyond sorrow. [N-186](../LEGACY-NOTES.md#n-186)[^G-U04312]

<a id="u04313"></a>

I have also seen the object of intrinsic nature's appearance.

<a id="u04314"></a>

Although I have gained confidence in the buddhas' enlightened intent,

<a id="u04315"></a>

for sentient beings who will enter in the future,

<a id="u04316"></a>

so that primordial knowing free from conceptual thought may be complete,

<a id="u04317"></a>

I seek the Array of the Nature of Phenomena in evenness. [N-186](../LEGACY-NOTES.md#n-186)

<a id="u04318"></a>

Where is the nature of phenomena of abiding to be applied?

<a id="u04319"></a>

What is the crucial point of the intervals of movement?

<a id="u04320"></a>

How is the nature of phenomena of time distinguished?

<a id="u04321"></a>

What is the nature of phenomena of buddhas like?

<a id="u04322"></a>

How does it abide at the time of sentient beings?

<a id="u04323"></a>

What are the crucial points of the body?

<a id="u04324"></a>

How does it emerge from the gates?

<a id="u04325"></a>

What are the characteristics of appearing in the object?

<a id="u04326"></a>

As what does it appear there? [N-186](../LEGACY-NOTES.md#n-186)

<a id="u04327"></a>

Having seen, by what is it decisively established?

<a id="u04328"></a>

Having arrived, what is the measure of completeness?

<a id="a2000-c04-s01"></a>

*Scan-restored main verses:*[^G-A2000-C04-S01]

When the measure is reached, what becomes of the body?<br />
What is ordinary mind like?<br />
What is the nature of phenomena when connection is severed?

<a id="u04329"></a>

By what is the nature of phenomena of cause and result accomplished?

<a id="u04330"></a>

What is the nature of phenomena that clears in its own place?

<a id="u04331"></a>

What are the definitive phrases of the nature of phenomena?

<a id="u04332"></a>

What are its crucial points?

<a id="u04333"></a>

How is this crucial point of the time of arising held?

<a id="u04334"></a>

What is the nature of phenomena of the intermediate state like?

<a id="u04335"></a>

How do its characteristics appear?

<a id="u04336"></a>

Speak of these for the benefit of sentient beings.’

<a id="u04337"></a>

Then, [the words of] the teacher Vajradhara,

<a id="u04338"></a>

from the space of pure intrinsic nature—

<a id="u04339"></a>

these elaborations of words and phrases—

<a id="u04340"></a>

arose in the Ground free from expression. [N-186](../LEGACY-NOTES.md#n-186)

<a id="u04341"></a>

‘Ema! Marvelous wonder!

<a id="u04342"></a>

I shall teach an expression of wonder

<a id="u04343"></a>

unheard of in other fields.

<a id="u04344"></a>

**[Source heading: First subject of inquiry.]**

<a id="u04345"></a>

The nature of phenomena of abiding is as follows:

<a id="u04346"></a>

abiding in its fundamental disposition, it is free from virtue and wrongdoing;

<a id="u04347"></a>

abiding as cause and result, it is free from dependence;

<a id="u04348"></a>

abiding through conditions, the elements are pure—

<a id="u04349"></a>

it does not shift or change. [N-187](../LEGACY-NOTES.md#n-187)

<a id="u04350"></a>

**[Source heading: Second subject of inquiry.]**

<a id="u04351"></a>

The nature of phenomena of the intervals of movement is like this:

<a id="u04352"></a>

I shall explain it fully as a great wonder.

<a id="u04353"></a>

Wind, according to the sequence of a day,

<a id="u04354"></a>

is reckoned as twenty thousand, one thousand,

<a id="u04355"></a>

and six hundred;

<a id="u04356"></a>

the winds of one day are thereby complete.

<a id="u04357"></a>

According to the particular distinctions,

<a id="u04358"></a>

for the young there are sixty extra;

<a id="u04359"></a>

for the old there is a shortfall.

<a id="u04360"></a>

This is through the activity of the elemental vessel. [N-188](../LEGACY-NOTES.md#n-188)

<a id="u04361"></a>

Among sixty movements of wind,

<a id="u04362"></a>

the subtle movement of the nature of phenomena is held through reckoning.

<a id="u04363"></a>

From two such groups it is coarse;

<a id="u04364"></a>

when these are gathered as three, it is gross.

<a id="u04365"></a>

Subtract three from that: it is even. [N-188](../LEGACY-NOTES.md#n-188)

<a id="u04366"></a>

In brief, within twenty movements,

<a id="u04367"></a>

there is one evenness of the nature of phenomena.

<a id="u04368"></a>

**[Source heading: Third subject of inquiry.]**

<a id="u04369"></a>

The nature of phenomena of time is as follows:

<a id="u04370"></a>

outer, inner, secret,

<a id="u04371"></a>

and suchness—four.

<a id="u04372"></a>

The outer is the second [month] of autumn,

<a id="u04373"></a>

showing the abiding of the nature of phenomena of time.

<a id="u04374"></a>

The inner is the time when dawn breaks.

<a id="u04375"></a>

The secret is sleep and dreams.

<a id="u04376"></a>

Suchness is held through wind.

<a id="u04377"></a>

When the time is reached, it is complete. [N-188](../LEGACY-NOTES.md#n-188)

<a id="u04378"></a>

**[Source heading: Fourth subject of inquiry.]**

<a id="u04379"></a>

The nature of phenomena of buddhas:

<a id="u04380"></a>

undivided, not dividing, even—

<a id="u04381"></a>

how could words, phrases, and names exist there?

<a id="u04382"></a>

The basis of dependence is self-purified, and delusion is purified.

<a id="u04383"></a>

It is not known by the names samsara and nirvana.

<a id="u04384"></a>

Not having come about or arisen, it does not cease.

<a id="u04385"></a>

All-arising primordial knowing is not any [thing]. [N-187](../LEGACY-NOTES.md#n-187)

<a id="u04386"></a>

There is no taking and no liberation.

<a id="u04387"></a>

In a moment free from causes and conditions,

<a id="u04388"></a>

buddhas and sentient beings appear as a foundation,

<a id="u04389"></a>

free from all multiplicity and parts. [N-187](../LEGACY-NOTES.md#n-187)

<a id="u04390"></a>

**[Source heading: Fifth subject of inquiry.]**

<a id="u04391"></a>

As for deluded sentient beings:

<a id="u04392"></a>

embodiments and primordial knowing are in their own manner,

<a id="u04393"></a>

genuine, fresh, and letting be—

<a id="u04394"></a>

abiding solely in ‘gyi nar’ [unresolved expression],

<a id="u04395"></a>

intrinsic nature at the faculties' objects. [N-189](../LEGACY-NOTES.md#n-189)

<a id="u04396"></a>

Here their own object is shown;

<a id="u04397"></a>

without entering into surrounding conditions,

<a id="u04398"></a>

in unobstructedness and penetration,

<a id="u04399"></a>

from the appearance aspects of object and knowing,

<a id="u04400"></a>

each is directly liberated from its own Ground. [N-189](../LEGACY-NOTES.md#n-189)

<a id="u04401"></a>

The desirable qualities are self-complete.

<a id="u04402"></a>

In the respective crucial points of diverse movement,

<a id="u04403"></a>

knowing one, one abides liberated.

<a id="u04404"></a>

Thus the nature of phenomena is realized,

<a id="u04405"></a>

free from parts.

<a id="u04406"></a>

**[Source heading: Sixth subject of inquiry.]**

<a id="u04407"></a>

The crucial points of the body are as follows:

<a id="u04408"></a>

the general [body], the heart, and the channels—

<a id="u04409"></a>

the pure, uncontrived nature of phenomena abides there. [N-190](../LEGACY-NOTES.md#n-190)

<a id="u04410"></a>

Throughout the bodies of all sentient beings,

<a id="u04411"></a>

the nature of phenomena pervades in the manner of wind.

<a id="u04412"></a>

Subtle, light, moving, and separating:

<a id="u04413"></a>

through these four ways of functioning,

<a id="u04414"></a>

know the yoga of the four times.

<a id="u04415"></a>

Within the heart it abides as embodiment.

<a id="u04416"></a>

The embodiments of radiance have five aspects;

<a id="u04417"></a>

they hold the characteristics of their respective families.

<a id="u04418"></a>

The six embodiments of rays

<a id="u04419"></a>

gather the phenomena of intrinsic nature's appearance.

<a id="u04420"></a>

Through the three aspects of pure embodiment,

<a id="u04421"></a>

the characteristics of passing beyond sorrow are held.

<a id="u04422"></a>

Through the eight aspect-embodiments,

<a id="u04423"></a>

the doing of completing grounds and paths is done. [N-190](../LEGACY-NOTES.md#n-190)

<a id="u04424"></a>

Within the channels are spheres.

<a id="u04425"></a>

Through three aspects of entering spheres,

<a id="u04426"></a>

the seeds connecting samsara and nirvana are planted.

<a id="u04427"></a>

Through the five arising spheres,

<a id="u04428"></a>

the mandala of yogic experience is established.

<a id="u04429"></a>

Through the six arraying spheres,

<a id="u04430"></a>

the manifold nature of phenomena is gathered into one.

<a id="u04431"></a>

Through reliance on each of these,

<a id="u04432"></a>

all phenomena have the identity of evenness. [N-190](../LEGACY-NOTES.md#n-190)

<a id="u04433"></a>

**[Source heading: Seventh reply.]**

<a id="u04434"></a>

As for how it emerges from the gates:

<a id="u04435"></a>

impelled by entering wind,

<a id="u04436"></a>

the four lamps become four activities.

<a id="u04437"></a>

Moved by pervading wind,

<a id="u04438"></a>

vajra chains [arise] from awareness itself.

<a id="u04439"></a>

Gathered by ripening wind,

<a id="u04440"></a>

they appear as embodiments in fivefold pairs. [N-191](../LEGACY-NOTES.md#n-191)

<a id="u04441"></a>

From the eyes, the two winds of apprehended object and apprehending subject:

<a id="u04442"></a>

through the right, the apprehended aspect, appearance increases;

<a id="u04443"></a>

through the left, the apprehending aspect, colors are complete. [N-191](../LEGACY-NOTES.md#n-191)

<a id="u04444"></a>

At the ears, from pervading and entering winds,

<a id="u04445"></a>

the right, through pervasion, displays sounds;

<a id="u04446"></a>

the left, through the activity of entering wind,

<a id="u04447"></a>

holds each respective [sound].

<a id="u04448"></a>

The wind of complete pervasion at the crown,

<a id="u04449"></a>

subtle and without generation,

<a id="u04450"></a>

also holds the embodiments of awareness. [N-191](../LEGACY-NOTES.md#n-191)

<a id="u04451"></a>

**[Source heading: Eighth reply.]**

<a id="u04452"></a>

The characteristics of appearing in the object:

<a id="u04453"></a>

from space, clear blue and unstirred,

<a id="u04454"></a>

light, colors, and shapes

<a id="u04455"></a>

display the self-appearance of the five primordial knowings.

<a id="u04456"></a>

From the lamp of pure basic space,

<a id="u04457"></a>

spheres, embodiments, and purified delusory appearance—

<a id="u04458"></a>

intrinsic nature itself and essence itself—

<a id="u04459"></a>

appear nondually, without joining or separating. [N-191](../LEGACY-NOTES.md#n-191)

<a id="u04460"></a>

At this time the characteristic of exhaustion

<a id="u04461"></a>

is just like space, free from doing.

<a id="u04462"></a>

**[Source heading: Ninth reply.]**

<a id="u04463"></a>

As for by what it is seen:

<a id="u04464"></a>

it is seen through familiarity and the crucial points.

<a id="u04465"></a>

Familiarity starts with the preliminaries;

<a id="u04466"></a>

applying the crucial points of body and voice,

<a id="u04467"></a>

one sees the forms of pure self-appearance,

<a id="u04468"></a>

and all delusion subsides.

<a id="u04469"></a>

Through the body at the crucial point, seeing occurs.

<a id="u04470"></a>

Through the lion posture of the dharma embodiment,

<a id="u04471"></a>

free from all fear of delusion,

<a id="u04472"></a>

one sees with the vajra eye.

<a id="u04473"></a>

Through the posture of the complete enjoyment embodiment,

<a id="u04474"></a>

relying on the reclining elephant,

<a id="u04475"></a>

one fully enjoys the nature of phenomena

<a id="u04476"></a>

and sees with the lotus eye.

<a id="u04477"></a>

Through the posture of the emanation embodiment,

<a id="u04478"></a>

relying on the crouching sage,

<a id="u04479"></a>

the nature of phenomena emanates of itself,

<a id="u04480"></a>

and one sees with the dharma eye. [N-192](../LEGACY-NOTES.md#n-192)

<a id="u04481"></a>

Furthermore, through channels and bodily exercises,

<a id="u04482"></a>

one sees the five primordial knowings of intrinsic nature's appearance

<a id="u04483"></a>

with the water-bubble eyes. [N-192](../LEGACY-NOTES.md#n-192)

<a id="u04484"></a>

**[Source heading: Tenth reply.]**

<a id="u04485"></a>

Having seen, one applies the crucial point.

<a id="u04486"></a>

Through methods of guiding appearance,

<a id="u04487"></a>

the appearances of the four elements reach the crucial point.

<a id="u04488"></a>

By reckoning the connections of the eight winds' activities,

<a id="u04489"></a>

manifold differentiating conceptualization reaches the crucial point.

<a id="u04490"></a>

For the body: compressing, shaking out expressiveness,

<a id="u04491"></a>

and the crucial points of striking, extending, and gathering the limbs;

<a id="u04492"></a>

through subtlety, straightening, and massage—

<a id="u04493"></a>

in flesh, blood, and bone. [N-192](../LEGACY-NOTES.md#n-192)

<a id="u04494"></a>

**[Source heading: Eleventh reply.]**

<a id="u04495"></a>

Having thus arrived, the measures of completeness are:

<a id="u04496"></a>

cessation of what appears directly before one;

<a id="u04497"></a>

introducing appearance into earth and stone;

<a id="u04498"></a>

one's own knowing entering anything;

<a id="u04499"></a>

through that entry, moving insentient things;

<a id="u04500"></a>

taking the measure of wind's movement; [N-193](../LEGACY-NOTES.md#n-193)

<a id="u04501"></a>

seeing the body's minute particles arises;

<a id="u04502"></a>

whatever appears is complete as form embodiment;

<a id="u04503"></a>

there, father and mother embrace;

<a id="u04504"></a>

fivefold fathers and mothers join;

<a id="u04505"></a>

all are within a round encircling band.

<a id="u04506"></a>

The yogin's body too is clear light;

<a id="u04507"></a>

going, coming, and abiding—

<a id="u04508"></a>

all are awareness reaching its measure. [N-194](../LEGACY-NOTES.md#n-194)

<a id="u04509"></a>

**[Source heading: Twelfth reply.]**

<a id="u04510"></a>

The manner of reaching the measure also becomes like this:

<a id="u04511"></a>

free from the particles of each body,

<a id="u04512"></a>

windows of light appear in the intervening space.

<a id="u04513"></a>

From these, rays shaped like hooks,

<a id="u04514"></a>

subtle and entwining, hold appearance.

<a id="u04515"></a>

The body's material weight self-ceases:

<a id="u04516"></a>

a stainless body of light with unimpeded penetration.

<a id="u04517"></a>

At its center is the sign of A,

<a id="u04518"></a>

and rays from the brow curl measure one armspan.

<a id="u04519"></a>

The topknot appears held back by wind. [N-194](../LEGACY-NOTES.md#n-194)[^G-U04519]

<a id="u04520"></a>

From the fingers of the peaceful embodiments,

<a id="u04521"></a>

garlands of light gather densely.

<a id="u04522"></a>

There is activity of ordinary mind without sound.

<a id="u04523"></a>

From the palace of the skull in the head,

<a id="u04524"></a>

Herukas manifestly arise.

<a id="u04525"></a>

At this time one's body is brought to its measure:

<a id="u04526"></a>

the three embodiments, with unimpeded penetration, gathered into one. [N-194](../LEGACY-NOTES.md#n-194)

<a id="u04527"></a>

**[Source heading: Thirteenth reply.]**

<a id="u04528"></a>

At this time ordinary mind is clear;

<a id="u04529"></a>

through the six higher knowings,

<a id="u04530"></a>

phenomena that are far away or measurable

<a id="u04531"></a>

are known in an instant.

<a id="u04532"></a>

Immeasurable buddha fields

<a id="u04533"></a>

and likewise embodiments appear to ordinary mind.

<a id="u04534"></a>

In empty ordinary mind, stains are exhausted.

<a id="u04535"></a>

Intrinsic nature does not move; emptiness and clarity are even.

<a id="u04536"></a>

Unmixed, complete in evenness, it pervades basic space.

<a id="u04537"></a>

Appearance and existence are clear, free from multiplicity.

<a id="u04538"></a>

Without parts, there is no elaboration. [N-195](../LEGACY-NOTES.md#n-195)

<a id="u04539"></a>

**[Source heading: Fourteenth reply.]**

<a id="u04540"></a>

At the times of such appearances,

<a id="u04541"></a>

the connection between body and ordinary mind is severed.

<a id="u04542"></a>

Even in the body whose contaminated [elements] are exhausted,

<a id="u04543"></a>

ordinary mind, characterized by clarity, emerges outward.

<a id="u04544"></a>

In the manner of a shooting star passing across,

<a id="u04545"></a>

it is clearly seen in the element of space. [N-195](../LEGACY-NOTES.md#n-195)

<a id="u04546"></a>

At the initial times of this,

<a id="u04547"></a>

even in separation from wind,

<a id="u04548"></a>

through effort and the activity of pure wind,

<a id="u04549"></a>

it arises from being projected outside the body.

<a id="u04550"></a>

Through the two aspects of what sees,

<a id="u04551"></a>

the body is pure clear light;

<a id="u04552"></a>

ordinary mind is like proliferating sparks,

<a id="u04553"></a>

not abiding in one place, a Ground of its own clarity.

<a id="u04554"></a>

It arises in the manner of two appearance aspects.

<a id="u04555"></a>

Through this, the connection between body and ordinary mind is severed,

<a id="u04556"></a>

and one does not return to the three realms. [N-195](../LEGACY-NOTES.md#n-195)

<a id="u04557"></a>

**[Source heading: Fifteenth reply.]**

<a id="u04558"></a>

The nature of phenomena of cause and result:

<a id="u04559"></a>

when the source of coarse conceptual thought has ceased,

<a id="u04560"></a>

in the continuum of self-purified mindfulness,

<a id="u04561"></a>

dependent connections and wind itself are completely pure.

<a id="u04562"></a>

It is the nature of phenomena arising from wind. [N-196](../LEGACY-NOTES.md#n-196)[^G-U04562]

<a id="u04563"></a>

In intrinsic nature, pure from the beginning,

<a id="u04564"></a>

the numbers one and many are exhausted.

<a id="u04565"></a>

There are no embodiments or primordial knowing;

<a id="u04566"></a>

having reached the limit where phenomena are exhausted,

<a id="u04567"></a>

this is held to be the nature of phenomena of the result. [N-196](../LEGACY-NOTES.md#n-196)

<a id="u04568"></a>

**[Source heading: Sixteenth reply.]**

<a id="u04569"></a>

The nature of phenomena that clears in its own place:

<a id="u04570"></a>

when the elements clear, material weight is purified;

<a id="u04571"></a>

particles, subtle and coarse, cease at their limit,

<a id="u04572"></a>

and not even a mere part abides.

<a id="u04573"></a>

When afflictions clear, delusion clears.

<a id="u04574"></a>

Free from conceptual thought, clinging does not abide;

<a id="u04575"></a>

neither dormancy nor manifest arising—

<a id="u04576"></a>

not even a particle's portion abides.

<a id="u04577"></a>

Through the clearing of subtle and coarse in this way,

<a id="u04578"></a>

the five aggregates gradually become subtle.

<a id="u04579"></a>

When the body of the four elements is exhausted,

<a id="u04580"></a>

all is embodiment complete in primordial knowing.

<a id="u04581"></a>

Deep absorption free from conceptualization abides of itself.

<a id="u04582"></a>

Beyond contrived phenomena,

<a id="u04583"></a>

it does not abide even in the phrase ‘phenomena.’ [N-196](../LEGACY-NOTES.md#n-196)

<a id="u04584"></a>

**[Source heading: Seventeenth reply.]**

<a id="u04585"></a>

The definitive phrase ‘nature of phenomena’ is thus:

<a id="u04586"></a>

‘phenomena’ means gathering and assembling,

<a id="u04587"></a>

doing doings and holding characteristics,

<a id="u04588"></a>

showing self-appearance

<a id="u04589"></a>

and holding the respective classes.

<a id="u04590"></a>

‘Nature’ means no contrivance;

<a id="u04591"></a>

abiding in letting be, it genuinely pervades.

<a id="u04592"></a>

Fresh, genuine, and in its original course,

<a id="u04593"></a>

the number of words, phrases, and names is exhausted. [N-197](../LEGACY-NOTES.md#n-197)

<a id="u04594"></a>

**[Source heading: Eighteenth reply.]**

<a id="u04595"></a>

The nature of phenomena of the crucial points is as follows:

<a id="u04596"></a>

the elements' abodes, one's body,

<a id="u04597"></a>

objects, gates, knowing, wind,

<a id="u04598"></a>

channels, spheres, and subsidiary aspects—

<a id="u04599"></a>

each respective entry abides pervasively.

<a id="u04600"></a>

Through transforming their respective preliminaries

<a id="u04601"></a>

and the abiding of the main practice,

<a id="u04602"></a>

in the nature of phenomena free from conceptual thought,

<a id="u04603"></a>

intrinsic nature abides and movement is exhausted.

<a id="u04604"></a>

From the object in which exhaustion and appearance are nondual,

<a id="u04605"></a>

through intrinsic abiding and contrived [practice],

<a id="u04606"></a>

the basic space of phenomena also appears through purity.

<a id="u04607"></a>

Through this, buddhahood comes through the crucial points. [N-197](../LEGACY-NOTES.md#n-197)

<a id="u04608"></a>

**[Source heading: Nineteenth reply.]**

<a id="u04609"></a>

The crucial point of the time of arising is like this:

<a id="u04610"></a>

Ema! I shall explain a great wonder.

<a id="u04611"></a>

This nature of phenomena has eight times of arising,

<a id="u04612"></a>

and just as many times of abiding.

<a id="u04613"></a>

Also at four distinctive times,

<a id="u04614"></a>

when the yogin's ordinary mind abides,

<a id="u04615"></a>

there is evenness without joining or separating.

<a id="u04616"></a>

Knowing the crucial point of pure intrinsic nature,

<a id="u04617"></a>

one knows phenomena without anything being done.

<a id="u04618"></a>

Time is held through the number of transitions;

<a id="u04619"></a>

through holding it, there is purity without delusion. [N-197](../LEGACY-NOTES.md#n-197)

**[Source heading: Twentieth reply, in full.]**

<a id="u04621"></a>

The nature of phenomena of the intermediate state is like this:

<a id="u04622"></a>

I shall explain the most secret nature of phenomena.

<a id="u04623"></a>

Distinguished, there are four aspects;

<a id="u04624"></a>

here I shall explain the intermediate state of the nature of phenomena.

<a id="u04625"></a>

When released from the moment of death,

<a id="u04626"></a>

consciousness dissolves into space.

<a id="u04627"></a>

When space dissolves into clear light,

<a id="u04628"></a>

all subtle and coarse appearances cease.

<a id="u04629"></a>

A body of light endowed with all faculties

<a id="u04630"></a>

becomes like a shadow from the body. [N-198](../LEGACY-NOTES.md#n-198)

<a id="u04631"></a>

At that time, when light appears in fragments,

<a id="u04632"></a>

the adept holds the spreading of appearance.

<a id="u04633"></a>

Through the spreading of moving wind,

<a id="u04634"></a>

light and rays of five colors

<a id="u04635"></a>

appear in uncertain rainbow patterns.

<a id="u04636"></a>

From entering and ripening winds,

<a id="u04637"></a>

the four directions, intermediate directions, above and below are established.

<a id="u04638"></a>

As the directions' lights gather there,

<a id="u04639"></a>

embodiments of the five families also manifest.

<a id="u04640"></a>

Blue, white, yellow, red, and green

<a id="u04641"></a>

Knowing how to spread and hold them,

one completes the buddhas' qualities

and does not enter the three realms. [N-198](../LEGACY-NOTES.md#n-198)

<a id="u04645"></a>

**[Source heading: Twenty-first reply.]**

<a id="u04646"></a>

Their characteristics are like this:

<a id="u04647"></a>

free from dependence on coarse elements,

<a id="u04648"></a>

spheres blaze as rays in the light.

<a id="u04649"></a>

Five connected spheres are single embodiments;

<a id="u04650"></a>

from half-embodiments, forms become complete.

<a id="u04651"></a>

From embodiments with half-forms, there are fivefold pairs.

<a id="u04652"></a>

As each cluster becomes complete,

<a id="u04653"></a>

there are groups of five, ten, and a hundred;

<a id="u04654"></a>

clusters of a thousand and a hundred thousand

<a id="u04655"></a>

appear pure in the intrinsic nature of what is envisaged. [N-198](../LEGACY-NOTES.md#n-198)

<a id="u04656"></a>

Innumerable, beyond what expression can encompass—

<a id="u04657"></a>

when one oneself knows their intrinsic nature,

<a id="u04658"></a>

one attains the three embodiments gathered into one.

<a id="u04659"></a>

From this attainment there is no return.

It comes from making this clear. [N-199](../LEGACY-NOTES.md#n-199)

<a id="u04661"></a>

Furthermore, the essence of the nature of phenomena

is established as essence from intrinsic nature itself.

<a id="u04663"></a>

Since its manner of appearance is not fixed as one,

<a id="u04664"></a>

the paths of entry also differ.

<a id="u04665"></a>

Through identity, knowing, and seeing liberation,

<a id="u04666"></a>

through entering, seeing, and familiarity—

<a id="u04667"></a>

from recognition, there is one's own identity;

<a id="u04668"></a>

through trust, one reaches it;

<a id="u04669"></a>

through decision, one enters confidence.

<a id="u04670"></a>

Through these three certainties,

<a id="u04671"></a>

essence being complete, the continuum ceases. [N-199](../LEGACY-NOTES.md#n-199)

<a id="u04672"></a>

Furthermore, I shall explain the intrinsic nature of the nature of phenomena.

<a id="u04673"></a>

Not fixed as one thing of this kind,

<a id="u04674"></a>

it appears however it is designated.

<a id="u04675"></a>

From the basis for assigning diverse names,

<a id="u04676"></a>

it appears as elaborations of many words and phrases.

<a id="u04677"></a>

Names are uncertain and diverse;

<a id="u04678"></a>

meaning too is empty through mere designation.

<a id="u04679"></a>

Since emptiness is not established as anything,

<a id="u04680"></a>

its intrinsic nature appears pure. [N-199](../LEGACY-NOTES.md#n-199)

<a id="u04681"></a>

Furthermore, I shall explain the compassionate responsiveness of the nature of phenomena.

<a id="u04682"></a>

Since the domains of those to be trained are diverse,

<a id="u04683"></a>

the embodiments that train them are likewise.

<a id="u04684"></a>

Through the transformations of classes and faculties,

<a id="u04685"></a>

conceptual thoughts of samsara and nirvana differ.

<a id="u04686"></a>

When each is left in its own object,

<a id="u04687"></a>

this is enlightened intent of the uncontrived meaning.

<a id="u04688"></a>

Furthermore, I shall explain the appearance of the nature of phenomena.

<a id="u04689"></a>

In impure delusion,

<a id="u04690"></a>

without contriving the gates of the faculties,

<a id="u04691"></a>

the crucial point is leaving [them] as they are;

<a id="u04692"></a>

not altering that is the pith instruction. [N-200](../LEGACY-NOTES.md#n-200)

<a id="u04693"></a>

In appearance without an apprehended object,

<a id="u04694"></a>

ordinary mind arises without an apprehending subject.

<a id="u04695"></a>

It is seen by eyes without activity;

<a id="u04696"></a>

drawn along a path without a nature of phenomena;

<a id="u04697"></a>

carried to a Ground without view and cultivation;

<a id="u04698"></a>

gathered into a result with nothing to do, free from effort.

<a id="u04699"></a>

Here the limits of appearance do not abide. [N-200](../LEGACY-NOTES.md#n-200)

<a id="u04700"></a>

Furthermore, I shall explain the nature of phenomena of awareness.

<a id="u04701"></a>

Through naked seeing in vajra chains,

<a id="u04702"></a>

the mindfulness of moving differentiating conceptualization ceases.

<a id="u04703"></a>

Like going in space along the birds' path,[^G-U04703]

<a id="u04704"></a>

there is nowhere to go from the basic space of phenomena.

<a id="u04705"></a>

With no other, awareness-appearance being completely pure,

<a id="u04706"></a>

the causes and conditions of delusive appearance are exhausted.

<a id="u04707"></a>

Since basic space and awareness are inseparable,

<a id="u04708"></a>

the connected appearance of vajra chains is shown.

<a id="u04709"></a>

The essence of awareness is vajra chains:

<a id="u04710"></a>

in past, future, or present,

<a id="u04711"></a>

no one has made them.

<a id="u04712"></a>

Thus being unconditioned

<a id="u04713"></a>

is the intrinsic nature of vajra chains.

<a id="u04714"></a>

All differentiating conceptualization being pure,

<a id="u04715"></a>

there is buddhahood without delusion. [N-200](../LEGACY-NOTES.md#n-200)

<a id="u04716"></a>

Furthermore, I shall explain the nature of phenomena of the continuum.

<a id="u04717"></a>

The true nature of phenomena penetrates beyond words;

<a id="u04718"></a>

its own essence is without letters.

<a id="u04719"></a>

The lamp-light of self-aware primordial knowing blazes.

<a id="u04720"></a>

With the nature of phenomena pure, auspiciousness is beautiful.

<a id="u04721"></a>

The view, free from fear, completes the lion's expressiveness;

<a id="u04722"></a>

with self-awareness ripened, completeness arises naturally;

<a id="u04723"></a>

vajra chains connect, a garland of pearls;

<a id="u04724"></a>

appearances of experience form an array of jeweled inlay;

<a id="u04725"></a>

self-abiding emptiness is the mirror of the core;

<a id="u04726"></a>

deviations are cut of themselves: the mirror of awakened mind.

<a id="u04727"></a>

Not arising from another, it is self-arising itself.

<a id="u04728"></a>

Because it arises naturally, there is self-liberation.

<a id="u04729"></a>

One in basic space, there are also six expanses.

<a id="u04730"></a>

With qualities complete, precious things are heaped.

<a id="u04731"></a>

Self-awareness being taught, it is adorned with pointing out.

<a id="u04732"></a>

With appearance pure, sun and moon are joined.

<a id="u04733"></a>

Because awareness ripens, embodiment-relics blaze.

<a id="u04734"></a>

All of these arise from the Ground. [N-201](../LEGACY-NOTES.md#n-201)

<a id="u04735"></a>

Thus, because the nature of phenomena is inexpressible,

<a id="u04736"></a>

it arises as the foundation of conventional phrases.

<a id="u04737"></a>

The sequences of expressed phrases being completely pure,

<a id="u04738"></a>

one thus abides in the inexpressible.

<a id="u04739"></a>

Therefore the limits of phrase-sequences are exhausted.

<a id="u04740"></a>

Furthermore, I shall explain the nature of phenomena of liberation.

<a id="u04741"></a>

Liberated through the crucial points, striving and effort are exhausted.

<a id="u04742"></a>

Liberated from the beginning, there is no need to repeat.

<a id="u04743"></a>

Because it is self-liberation, there is no antidote.

<a id="u04744"></a>

Because it is naked liberation, it vanishes where it is seen.

<a id="u04745"></a>

Completely liberated, intrinsic nature is pure.

<a id="u04746"></a>

Liberated through time, there is no need for familiarity.

<a id="u04747"></a>

Liberated through intrinsic nature, it is uncontrived.

<a id="u04748"></a>

‘Liberated’ is merely a convention.

<a id="u04749"></a>

Whose phenomena are realization and non-realization?

<a id="u04750"></a>

For whom there is ‘liberated,’ what is the object of focus? [N-202](../LEGACY-NOTES.md#n-202)

<a id="u04751"></a>

For whom could entry into the three realms occur?

<a id="u04752"></a>

This is the nature of phenomena liberated from the limits of existence.

<a id="u04753"></a>

Furthermore, the nature of phenomena free from doing:

<a id="u04754"></a>

with no doing, there is liberation;

<a id="u04755"></a>

with no arising, there is no abiding.

<a id="u04756"></a>

With no going, coming and circling are exhausted.

<a id="u04757"></a>

There is neither one nor two of what is done or arisen.

<a id="u04758"></a>

There is not even mere conventional delusion.

<a id="u04759"></a>

It is empty of entry into activities of existence and nonexistence.

<a id="u04760"></a>

From the beginning, the result is free from the conceptual mind,

<a id="u04761"></a>

beyond all speaking, thinking, and expression. [N-203](../LEGACY-NOTES.md#n-203)

<a id="u04762"></a>

Thus, from the Great All-Penetrating Word, Root of All Phenomena:

<a id="u04763"></a>

the fourth chapter, the Array of the Nature of Phenomena, teaching the root of ordinary mind's appearance.[^G-U04763]
