<a id="u02637"></a>

<a id="u03533"></a>

Also, from the numerical measures of wind,

<a id="u03534"></a>

one knows through the number one hundred and twenty.

<a id="u03535"></a>

Some rely on objects,

<a id="u03536"></a>

from which distinctions of true and false arise.

<a id="u03537"></a>

Some, from having no object of focus,

<a id="u03538"></a>

are to be known through mere neutrality.

<a id="u03539"></a>

Some desire samsara and nirvana as well;

<a id="u03540"></a>

from the two truths, two ordinary minds become deluded. [N-155](../LEGACY-NOTES.md#n-155)

<a id="u03541"></a>

From this, appearing conditions, afflictions, and so forth.

<a id="u03542"></a>

Furthermore, what is to be closely explained:

<a id="u03543"></a>

the intrinsic nature of delusive appearance

<a id="u03544"></a>

was not previously seen to be primordial knowing.

<a id="u03545"></a>

Because there is no Ground of delusion accompanied by conceptual thought,

<a id="u03546"></a>

the all-basis is realized as the great dharma embodiment.

<a id="u03547"></a>

The flow of delusion's own conceptual thought is cut.

<a id="u03548"></a>

Thus, undeluded appearance

<a id="u03549"></a>

arises from the crucial point of liberation from the outset. [N-155](../LEGACY-NOTES.md#n-155)

<a id="u03550"></a>

The aggregates of beings are the buddhas' embodiments:

<a id="u03551"></a>

who has not known them to be so from the outset?

<a id="u03552"></a>

As for the aggregates having the identity of the five buddhas,

<a id="u03553"></a>

[this] arises from the accomplishment of letters abiding in the channels.

<a id="u03554"></a>

From the bubble-like eyes, primordial knowing—

<a id="u03555"></a>

who has not seen it to be self-appearance?

<a id="u03556"></a>

From this, delusive appearance

<a id="u03557"></a>

arises from the crucial point of the lamps. [N-156](../LEGACY-NOTES.md#n-156)

<a id="u03558"></a>

Furthermore, because bodily conduct itself

<a id="u03559"></a>

is liberated as the three embodiments,

<a id="u03560"></a>

all actions and agents are the nature of phenomena.

<a id="u03561"></a>

Whatever is spoken through speech

<a id="u03562"></a>

is the secret recitation of Secret Mantra,

<a id="u03563"></a>

arising from the crucial point of the letters' magical displays.

<a id="u03564"></a>

All the conceptual thoughts of beings' ordinary minds—

<a id="u03565"></a>

why are they not known to be meditative stabilization?

<a id="u03566"></a>

Because one is liberated through realizing one's own ordinary mind,

<a id="u03567"></a>

this arises from the crucial point of the elements.

<a id="u03568"></a>

Thus, in the nature of phenomena of everything,

<a id="u03569"></a>

there is nothing whatsoever to be trained in,

<a id="u03570"></a>

and not the slightest thing to be placed.

<a id="u03571"></a>

It is the nature of phenomena with nothing above it.

<a id="u03572"></a>

From actions and agents, expression and reflection,

<a id="u03573"></a>

examine the crucial point of liberating samsara and nirvana.

<a id="u03574"></a>

There is not even one who is not a buddha.

<a id="u03575"></a>

The samsara of the three realms is emptied from its depths. [N-156](../LEGACY-NOTES.md#n-156)

<a id="u03576"></a>

Furthermore, all sentient beings of the three realms,

<a id="u03577"></a>

because they do not go beyond body, speech, and mental faculty,

<a id="u03578"></a>

need not seek the three embodiments elsewhere.

<a id="u03579"></a>

Even if sought, there is no place where they are found.

<a id="u03580"></a>

From what has not arisen and has no arising,

<a id="u03581"></a>

seeing through looking is greatly wondrous.

<a id="u03582"></a>

In the play of great wonder,

<a id="u03583"></a>

there is no division into buddhas and sentient beings.

<a id="u03584"></a>

Like clouds in space,

<a id="u03585"></a>

naturally arising, complete in themselves, they subside.

<a id="u03586"></a>

Through the distinction of directly perceiving the nature of phenomena,

<a id="u03587"></a>

because faculties are neither sharp nor dull,

<a id="u03588"></a>

all sentient beings do not abide

<a id="u03589"></a>

elsewhere than as buddhas.

<a id="u03590"></a>

Because those with bodies are pervaded by ordinary mind,

<a id="u03591"></a>

not a buddha—not a sentient being. [N-157](../LEGACY-NOTES.md#n-157)

<a id="u03592"></a>

Like a result from a seed,

<a id="u03593"></a>

the crucial point of the condition of one's own intrinsic nature,

<a id="u03594"></a>

not through words of expression,

<a id="u03595"></a>

but through clear appearance in the objects of the faculties,

<a id="u03596"></a>

is the identity of liberation through naked seeing. [N-157](../LEGACY-NOTES.md#n-157)

<a id="u03597"></a>

Furthermore, through conditions, among sentient beings,

<a id="u03598"></a>

there is not even one who is not a buddha.

<a id="u03599"></a>

Because self-appearance and primordial knowing accord as phenomena,

<a id="u03600"></a>

samsara has not existed from the outset.

<a id="u03601"></a>

Therefore, each is a buddha.

<a id="u03602"></a>

Birth itself is from conceptual thought.

<a id="u03603"></a>

Abiding in the womb is the basic space of phenomena.

<a id="u03604"></a>

Through body and ordinary mind gathering, basic space and awareness are connected.

<a id="u03605"></a>

Through abiding in a body, there are the three embodiments.

<a id="u03606"></a>

Through aging, phenomena are exhausted and delusive appearances stop.

<a id="u03607"></a>

Through illness, there is experiential acquaintance with phenomena.

<a id="u03608"></a>

Through death, there is emptiness without identification.

<a id="u03609"></a>

Therefore, sentient beings are buddhas. [N-157](../LEGACY-NOTES.md#n-157)

<a id="u03610"></a>

Furthermore, the crucial point of directly perceiving the nature of phenomena:

<a id="u03611"></a>

through seeing alone, there is knowing;

<a id="u03612"></a>

through knowing, there is realization;

<a id="u03613"></a>

through realization alone, one is liberated.

<a id="u03614"></a>

What is called liberated is not something unbound by an agent.

<a id="u03615"></a>

Just as space itself, into space,

<a id="u03616"></a>

dissolves, with intrinsic nature without an object of focus,

<a id="u03617"></a>

there is no partiality or portion.

<a id="u03618"></a>

For the fortunate who see directly,

<a id="u03619"></a>

because even the name ‘samsara of the three realms’ is absent,

<a id="u03620"></a>

the three existences are decisively resolved. [N-158](../LEGACY-NOTES.md#n-158)

<a id="u03621"></a>

Thus, from The Great All-Penetrating Word, the Root of All Phenomena,

<a id="u03622"></a>

this is the second chapter: ‘Array of Gathered Crucial Points: The Definite Arising of the Root of Awareness.’ [N-158](../LEGACY-NOTES.md#n-158)[^G-U03622][^G-C2-BOUNDARY-METADATA]
