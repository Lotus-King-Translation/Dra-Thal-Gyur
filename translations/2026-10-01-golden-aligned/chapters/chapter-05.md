## Chapter 5

<a id="u04764"></a>

Then, through completely pure intrinsic nature,

<a id="u04765"></a>

from the inexpressible dharma embodiment,

<a id="u04766"></a>

from the space of the nature of phenomena, free from conceptual elaborations,

<a id="u04767"></a>

from the inexpressible foundation,

<a id="u04768"></a>

phrases of meaning in a naturally arisen array

<a id="u04769"></a>

arose like light proliferating from the teacher's tongue,

<a id="u04770"></a>

without being made by anyone. [N-204](../LEGACY-NOTES.md#n-204)

<a id="u04771"></a>

The Lord of Gods supplicated:

<a id="u04772"></a>

‘Kyema! Blessed one, lord without delusion,

<a id="u04773"></a>

by whom will the Array of View and Cultivation

<a id="u04774"></a>

be held?

<a id="u04775"></a>

What is the natural state established in its own disposition?

<a id="u04776"></a>

How should one view it?

<a id="u04777"></a>

Distinguish the boundary between viewing phrases and meaning.

<a id="u04778"></a>

What are the essence and intrinsic nature of view?

<a id="u04779"></a>

What are the divisions of expressed phrases and names?

<a id="u04780"></a>

What is that called cultivation like?

<a id="u04781"></a>

What are its activity and understanding?

<a id="u04782"></a>

What cultivation abides without deviation?

<a id="u04783"></a>

How do cultivation's characteristics appear?

<a id="u04784"></a>

What are the faults of not cultivating?

<a id="u04785"></a>

I wish to hear these.

<a id="u04786"></a>

Vajra-holder, speak to me.’

<a id="u04787"></a>

Then, from uncontrived space,

<a id="u04788"></a>

the one bearing thirty-two marks

<a id="u04789"></a>

spoke the Array of View and Cultivation thus:

<a id="u04790"></a>

‘Lord of Gods, listen well.

<a id="u04791"></a>

Whoever doubts this

<a id="u04792"></a>

obscures the embodiments of the buddhas.

<a id="u04793"></a>

**[Source heading: First reply.]**

<a id="u04794"></a>

Those who hold view and cultivation

<a id="u04795"></a>

have excellent faith and diligence,

<a id="u04796"></a>

are able to give and respect the lama,

<a id="u04797"></a>

possess disciplined observance and avoid wrongdoing;

<a id="u04798"></a>

unchanging and utterly stable, they hold it.

<a id="u04799"></a>

Alternatively, one whose own impetus is quiet,

<a id="u04800"></a>

letting be, with a simple person's relaxation,

<a id="u04801"></a>

whose realization is small and whose intrinsic nature is quiet,

<a id="u04802"></a>

who is free from doings of body and voice,

<a id="u04803"></a>

and possesses discerning knowing, will hold it. [N-205](../LEGACY-NOTES.md#n-205)

<a id="u04804"></a>

**[Source heading: Second reply.]**

<a id="u04805"></a>

The natural state established in its own disposition

<a id="u04806"></a>

also has seven manners of abiding:

<a id="u04807"></a>

spontaneous presence from the side of diversity;

<a id="u04808"></a>

indeterminacy from the aspect of the extent of change;

<a id="u04809"></a>

certainty through not changing;

<a id="u04810"></a>

appearance's expressiveness transforming into anything;

<a id="u04811"></a>

all arising, together with affirmation;

<a id="u04812"></a>

primordial purity because delusion is purified;

<a id="u04813"></a>

and the variegation held to be diverse manners of appearance. [N-206](../LEGACY-NOTES.md#n-206)

<a id="u04814"></a>

Through such distinctions in what is asserted,

<a id="u04815"></a>

because the Great Perfection appears as anything,

<a id="u04816"></a>

although differing assertions are numerous,

<a id="u04817"></a>

in the view of abiding in its own disposition

<a id="u04818"></a>

there is no one, no two, and freedom from anything.

<a id="u04819"></a>

Even taking life and taking what is not given

<a id="u04820"></a>

arise from me and are my phenomena. [N-207](../LEGACY-NOTES.md#n-207)

<a id="u04821"></a>

View, cultivation, and activity too

<a id="u04822"></a>

are beyond the extremes of existence, nonexistence, and emptiness.

<a id="u04823"></a>

In brief, they are distinguished through characteristics.

<a id="u04824"></a>

**[Source heading: Third reply.]**

<a id="u04825"></a>

There are two ways of viewing this:

<a id="u04826"></a>

viewing phrases and viewing meaning.

<a id="u04827"></a>

Viewing phrases has seven stages;

<a id="u04828"></a>

their respective assertions are constructed by the conceptual mind:

<a id="u04829"></a>

freedom from doing and absence of striving;

<a id="u04830"></a>

gradual entry and drawing out distinctions of self and other;

<a id="u04831"></a>

taking life, and freedom from virtue and wrongdoing;

<a id="u04832"></a>

nothing done and nothing arisen;

<a id="u04833"></a>

unaltered appearance, self-purified;

<a id="u04834"></a>

and afflictions held to be liberated in intrinsic nature. [N-207](../LEGACY-NOTES.md#n-207)

<a id="u04835"></a>

Meaning is viewed in direct perception of the nature of phenomena,

<a id="u04836"></a>

not through discerning knowing,

<a id="u04837"></a>

but as the very domain of the faculties.

<a id="u04838"></a>

It is not decisively reached through phrases.

<a id="u04839"></a>

In the identity of seeing the three crucial points,

<a id="u04840"></a>

because the three phrases are explained as crucial points,

<a id="u04841"></a>

there are no sharp or dull faculties.

<a id="u04842"></a>

Because seeing the nature of phenomena purifies,

<a id="u04843"></a>

there is no good or bad in activities.

<a id="u04844"></a>

The nature of phenomena is empty and without divisions;

<a id="u04845"></a>

virtue and wrongdoing, both, are removed. [N-207](../LEGACY-NOTES.md#n-207)

<a id="u04846"></a>

Without seeing self-awareness directly,

<a id="u04847"></a>

the assertions of the nine stages do not liberate.

<a id="u04848"></a>

When the nature of phenomena is exhausted and its limit reached,

<a id="u04849"></a>

the three embodiments are the appearance of the path.

<a id="u04850"></a>

**[Source heading: Fourth reply.]**

<a id="u04851"></a>

The boundary between the views of phrases and meaning:

<a id="u04852"></a>

phrases are causes of expression;

<a id="u04853"></a>

whatever expresses or produces an emanation

<a id="u04854"></a>

is mental examination that thinks about it.

<a id="u04855"></a>

Meaning is oneself viewing oneself.

<a id="u04856"></a>

Through the three measures of direct perception,

<a id="u04857"></a>

one engages directly with vajra chains.

<a id="u04858"></a>

Through naked seeing and naked liberation,

<a id="u04859"></a>

whatever one sees is meaning. [N-208](../LEGACY-NOTES.md#n-208)

<a id="u04860"></a>

**[Source heading: Fifth reply.]**

<a id="u04861"></a>

The essence of view is seeing,

<a id="u04862"></a>

unchanged by phrases of mental examination.

<a id="u04863"></a>

Neither authoritative transmission nor the lama's pith instructions

<a id="u04864"></a>

shows it by any [means]. [N-208](../LEGACY-NOTES.md#n-208)

<a id="u04865"></a>

**[Source heading: Sixth reply.]**

<a id="u04866"></a>

The intrinsic nature of view pervades environment and inhabitants;

<a id="u04867"></a>

nothing is unpervaded, so anything is liberated.

<a id="u04868"></a>

In liberated phenomena, differentiated grasping is absent.

<a id="u04869"></a>

Whatever appears is one's own nature of phenomena;

<a id="u04870"></a>

there is no separate contrived nature of phenomena.

<a id="u04871"></a>

However one acts, non-conceptuality arises.

<a id="u04872"></a>

There is self-appearance without rejecting and accepting.

<a id="u04873"></a>

**[Source heading: Seventh reply.]**

<a id="u04874"></a>

The divisions of expressed phrases and names

<a id="u04875"></a>

are outer, inner, secret,

<a id="u04876"></a>

and unsurpassed—the four views.

<a id="u04877"></a>

The outer is viewing bearers of properties:

<a id="u04878"></a>

in the respective assertions of the eight stages,

<a id="u04879"></a>

mental examination and grasping are maintained. [N-209](../LEGACY-NOTES.md#n-209)

<a id="u04880"></a>

The inner: viewing the nature of phenomena,

<a id="u04881"></a>

one sees essence and intrinsic nature as illusion.

<a id="u04882"></a>

The secret: viewing self-awareness,

<a id="u04883"></a>

one sees its uninterrupted connection with vajra chains.

<a id="u04884"></a>

Suchness is basic space and awareness. [N-209](../LEGACY-NOTES.md#n-209)

<a id="u04885"></a>

**[Source heading: Eighth reply.]**

<a id="u04886"></a>

What is called cultivation is ordinary mind abiding,

<a id="u04887"></a>

cutting outer and inner movement,

<a id="u04888"></a>

and ceasing apprehended object and apprehending subject.

<a id="u04889"></a>

What arises from intrinsic nature's own disposition

<a id="u04890"></a>

is to be called meditative stabilization.

<a id="u04891"></a>

Wind, channels, spheres,

<a id="u04892"></a>

the body's crucial points, the eyes, and emptiness—

<a id="u04893"></a>

these are held to be cultivation.

<a id="u04894"></a>

The light and colors of letters,

<a id="u04895"></a>

and the emanations' proliferating and gathering,

<a id="u04896"></a>

are explained as deep absorption.

<a id="u04897"></a>

Uncontrived, resting of itself, is the natural state.

<a id="u04898"></a>

Because there is no change from that, however [it occurs],

<a id="u04899"></a>

it is the buddhas' enlightened intent. [N-210](../LEGACY-NOTES.md#n-210)

<a id="u04900"></a>

**[Source heading: Ninth reply.]**

<a id="u04901"></a>

Its activity and understanding:

<a id="u04902"></a>

through activity, clinging to objects is cut,

<a id="u04903"></a>

afflictions and the like are rejected,

<a id="u04904"></a>

and the root of samsara is drawn out.

<a id="u04905"></a>

Through understanding, the nature of ordinary mind is held,

<a id="u04906"></a>

made to abide, and wind is exhausted;

<a id="u04907"></a>

the path of the empty nature of phenomena is found,

<a id="u04908"></a>

and there is ground-appearance of pure intrinsic nature.

<a id="u04909"></a>

**[Source heading: Tenth reply.]**

<a id="u04910"></a>

Cultivation that abides without deviation:

<a id="u04911"></a>

the faculties' gates are clear,

<a id="u04912"></a>

and the mental faculty's conceptual thought does not move.

<a id="u04913"></a>

The mental faculty that examines knowing itself

<a id="u04914"></a>

does not escape outward.

<a id="u04915"></a>

It is not a clarity marked by divisions;

<a id="u04916"></a>

it has not gone into an emptiness cut off separately;

<a id="u04917"></a>

it is not a continuous succession of bliss;

<a id="u04918"></a>

it has not become a stagnant pool of equanimity. [N-211](../LEGACY-NOTES.md#n-211)

<a id="u04919"></a>

Resting in clear mindfulness and awareness;

<a id="u04920"></a>

training's contrary aspects having become the path;

<a id="u04921"></a>

an apprehending aspect with a continuous inward ease;

<a id="u04922"></a>

an apprehending aspect clear in its manner of parts;

<a id="u04923"></a>

and casting out colors and syllables—[^G-U04923]

<a id="u04924"></a>

when one has not fallen to the side of any of these,

<a id="u04925"></a>

this is cultivation abiding without deviation. [N-211](../LEGACY-NOTES.md#n-211)

<a id="u04926"></a>

**[Source heading: Eleventh reply.]**

<a id="u04927"></a>

The characteristics and manner of appearance of cultivation:

<a id="u04928"></a>

its characteristic is empty, clear awareness;

<a id="u04929"></a>

its intrinsic nature is mixed with nothing;

<a id="u04930"></a>

the limits of apprehended object and apprehending subject are exhausted;

<a id="u04931"></a>

and the nature of phenomena is pure in its own identity.

<a id="u04932"></a>

Its manner of appearance is not delusion.

<a id="u04933"></a>

In the intrinsic nature of pure causes and conditions,

<a id="u04934"></a>

things and absence of things are self-purified;

<a id="u04935"></a>

clinging to the objects of the five gates is purified;

<a id="u04936"></a>

the order of the elements is purified;

<a id="u04937"></a>

and the increasing appearance of pure primordial knowing is shown.

<a id="u04938"></a>

There is the nature of phenomena of exhaustion. [N-212](../LEGACY-NOTES.md#n-212)

<a id="u04939"></a>

**[Source heading: Twelfth reply.]**

<a id="u04940"></a>

The faults of not cultivating are:

<a id="u04941"></a>

the characteristics of samsara manifest;

<a id="u04942"></a>

self and other, objects and knowing,

<a id="u04943"></a>

a view bound to phrases,

<a id="u04944"></a>

and objects bound to focus—

<a id="u04945"></a>

afflictions bind [one] as a self.

<a id="u04946"></a>

The path of buddhahood is also lost;

<a id="u04947"></a>

the intrinsic nature of the result is not known;

<a id="u04948"></a>

there is no Ground of the evenness of all phenomena;

<a id="u04949"></a>

self-awareness is bound in the three realms;

<a id="u04950"></a>

and one falls into what is accompanied by conceptual thought. [N-212](../LEGACY-NOTES.md#n-212)

<a id="u04951"></a>

**[Source heading: Below this, the condensed meaning is explained.]**

<a id="u04952"></a>

Furthermore, I shall explain the wheel of the nature of phenomena.

<a id="u04953"></a>

Through the view of abiding, all phenomena are realized.

<a id="u04954"></a>

With intrinsic nature pure, samsara and nirvana are distinguished.

<a id="u04955"></a>

With nothing done, there is arising.

<a id="u04956"></a>

Brought onto the true path, it pervades.

<a id="u04957"></a>

Completely arisen, the nature of phenomena is empty.

<a id="u04958"></a>

With no doing, non-arising is complete.

<a id="u04959"></a>

Samsara being purified, there is passing beyond sorrow.

<a id="u04960"></a>

Furthermore, the view of pure seeing:

<a id="u04961"></a>

the great completeness of self-purified absence of things.

<a id="u04962"></a>

There is no need to make offerings to deities;

<a id="u04963"></a>

there is no mantra recitation or austerity;

<a id="u04964"></a>

no incense, lamps, or food offerings to deities;

<a id="u04965"></a>

no gathering offerings or offerings to the lama;

<a id="u04966"></a>

no praise or burnt offerings;

<a id="u04967"></a>

no scope of activity of the six perfections.

<a id="u04968"></a>

Thus, with no Ground or root whatsoever:

<a id="u04969"></a>

taking life, taking what is not given, and unchaste activity— [N-213](../LEGACY-NOTES.md#n-213)

<a id="u04970"></a>

there is no view, no cultivation, and no activity.

<a id="u04971"></a>

With nothing to view or enact, what assertion of cultivation is there?

<a id="u04972"></a>

Ordinary mind's assertions of emptiness and clarity cease.

<a id="u04973"></a>

Likewise, phenomena and the phenomena of buddhahood are absent. [N-213](../LEGACY-NOTES.md#n-213)

<a id="u04974"></a>

Furthermore, the view of intrinsic nature:

<a id="u04975"></a>

the Great Perfection of intrinsic nature, without things.

<a id="u04976"></a>

Earth, water, fire, and wind—the elements—are manifestly buddha.

<a id="u04977"></a>

Without contrivance, there is great self-liberation itself.

<a id="u04978"></a>

Desire, hatred, dull confusion, and awakening—four. [N-214](../LEGACY-NOTES.md#n-214)

<a id="u04979"></a>

The activity of differentiating conceptualization and karma is confidence in realization.

<a id="u04980"></a>

Without rejecting samsara, there is the buddhas' enlightened intent.

<a id="u04981"></a>

Body, voice, and mental faculty being refined, the three embodiments are complete.

<a id="u04982"></a>

Ordinary mind, ignorance, and delusion are the Ground of the nature of phenomena.

<a id="u04983"></a>

Karma, habitual tendencies, and clinging are crucial points of view. [N-214](../LEGACY-NOTES.md#n-214)

<a id="u04984"></a>

Furthermore, the contrived view:

<a id="u04985"></a>

for a buddha that has not arisen from phenomena,

<a id="u04986"></a>

a result complete without being generated by a cause,

<a id="u04987"></a>

one takes a body not destroyed by conditions.

<a id="u04988"></a>

With tenets not arising from authoritative transmission,

<a id="u04989"></a>

and pith instructions not relying on the continuum,

<a id="u04990"></a>

one cuts the root of passing beyond sorrow,

<a id="u04991"></a>

and makes the result of samsara ripen.

<a id="u04992"></a>

An untrue, impure Ground—

<a id="u04993"></a>

in the foundation of the six afflictions,

<a id="u04994"></a>

the seed of awakening is emptied out.

<a id="u04995"></a>

Forms of the three lower destinies ripen,

<a id="u04996"></a>

and the three embodiments too are ‘lag bcangs’ [unresolved]. [N-215](../LEGACY-NOTES.md#n-215)

<a id="u04997"></a>

Furthermore, I shall explain the view of what is expressed.

<a id="u04998"></a>

If life is not cut, one does not become buddha. [N-216](../LEGACY-NOTES.md#n-216)

<a id="u04999"></a>

If the windows are not closed,

<a id="u05000"></a>

the darkness itself is not cleared. [N-216](../LEGACY-NOTES.md#n-216)

<a id="u05001"></a>

The teacher of the buddhas,

<a id="u05002"></a>

at the king of Dharma in hell,

<a id="u05003"></a>

leads through Dharma to the three lower destinies.

<a id="u05004"></a>

Through practicing wrongdoing, virtue is attained. [N-217](../LEGACY-NOTES.md#n-217)

<a id="u05005"></a>

Through viewing, [one enters] samsara in the three realms;

<a id="u05006"></a>

through cultivating, one gathers all the hosts of afflictions;

<a id="u05007"></a>

through enacting activity, [one enters] deviation and obscuration;

<a id="u05008"></a>

the result is the very cause of samsara. [N-217](../LEGACY-NOTES.md#n-217)

<a id="u05009"></a>

Furthermore, I shall explain the connection of symbolic meaning and phrases.

<a id="u05010"></a>

In the midst of an expanse of the conflagration of an age,

<a id="u05011"></a>

a person, without being burned,

<a id="u05012"></a>

expounds the Dharma of the scriptural collections from his mouth,

<a id="u05013"></a>

while in action cutting another's life—

<a id="u05014"></a>

and his virtuous practice increases. How strange! [N-217](../LEGACY-NOTES.md#n-217)

<a id="u05015"></a>

Inside a spacious iron house without a door,

<a id="u05016"></a>

in the midst of an expanse of lightless darkness,

<a id="u05017"></a>

although sun, moon, and clear light are there,

<a id="u05018"></a>

no one sees them.

<a id="u05019"></a>

The objects of each one's faculties are clear,

<a id="u05020"></a>

yet there is no seeing. How strange! [N-217](../LEGACY-NOTES.md#n-217)

<a id="u05021"></a>

Furthermore, I shall explain the view that apprehends objects.

<a id="u05022"></a>

Emptying the Ground of samsara in the three realms,

<a id="u05023"></a>

one sweeps away passing beyond sorrow.

<a id="u05024"></a>

Cutting the root of ignorance and samsara,

<a id="u05025"></a>

one uproots the result of the three embodiments.

<a id="u05026"></a>

Putting on the armor of darkness, one extinguishes the sun;

<a id="u05027"></a>

the clear means of seeing is covered by darkness.

<a id="u05028"></a>

Giving an edge its sharp temper, not even one [thing] is cut.

<a id="u05029"></a>

With great breadth and an empty center, not even one [thing] fits.

<a id="u05030"></a>

Gathering diversity, one is not found.

<a id="u05031"></a>

Conceptual thought at the extremes of existence and nonexistence is cleared. [N-218](../LEGACY-NOTES.md#n-218)

<a id="u05032"></a>

Furthermore, I shall explain the view of desiring ordinary mind.

<a id="u05033"></a>

Striking all, not even one is cut;

<a id="u05034"></a>

striking one, all are to be killed.

<a id="u05035"></a>

A hundred people in iron fetters—not one is freed;

<a id="u05036"></a>

holding one, all are freed.

<a id="u05037"></a>

Through freedom, the path of liberation above is exhausted.

<a id="u05038"></a>

The cause of entering activity ceases. [N-218](../LEGACY-NOTES.md#n-218)

<a id="u05039"></a>

Furthermore, I shall explain the wheel of activity.

<a id="u05040"></a>

Since there is no arising, there is no self;

<a id="u05041"></a>

neither taking nor casting away is the seal of embodiment.

<a id="u05042"></a>

Do not search for elements; do not reject what exists.

<a id="u05043"></a>

Arouse desire, but do not become attached.

<a id="u05044"></a>

Having become attached, let go.

<a id="u05045"></a>

Do not disparage another's body or set [your own] above it.

<a id="u05046"></a>

Do not interrupt the flow of mantra and gestures.

<a id="u05047"></a>

Do not reject tenets; do not reject Dharma.

<a id="u05048"></a>

Do not disparage companions or the lama. [N-219](../LEGACY-NOTES.md#n-219)

<a id="u05049"></a>

Furthermore, I shall explain pledges and vows.

<a id="u05050"></a>

Lamas and buddhas are supports for offerings;

<a id="u05051"></a>

to Dharma, the community, chosen deities,

<a id="u05052"></a>

and vajra ḍākinīs,

<a id="u05053"></a>

do not cut the flow of gathering offerings and other offerings.

<a id="u05054"></a>

On seeing a lama endowed with the characteristics,

<a id="u05055"></a>

make praise and offerings complete.

<a id="u05056"></a>

Do not disparage women; do not become angry.

<a id="u05057"></a>

Do not disparage the aggregates; rely on the chosen deity.

<a id="u05058"></a>

Relying on mandalas, mantras, deities,

<a id="u05059"></a>

and gestures is authentic.

<a id="u05060"></a>

Do not break up gatherings of the faithful;

<a id="u05061"></a>

do not disturb the ordinary minds of those possessing virtue.

<a id="u05062"></a>

Possessing vows, abide in the pledges. [N-219](../LEGACY-NOTES.md#n-219)

<a id="u05063"></a>

Furthermore, the stages of training:

<a id="u05064"></a>

complete the body's activity,

<a id="u05065"></a>

and thoroughly cut speech.

<a id="u05066"></a>

Let ordinary mind abide, and examine and investigate it.

<a id="u05067"></a>

In the stages of progressively entering activities,

<a id="u05068"></a>

know the measures and characteristics.

<a id="u05069"></a>

Constantly, through ointments and pills,

<a id="u05070"></a>

introduce the channels into a net of spheres. [N-220](../LEGACY-NOTES.md#n-220)

<a id="u05071"></a>

Garlands, necklaces, and half-[necklaces] too,

<a id="u05072"></a>

beautiful and encircling, are to be connected.

<a id="u05073"></a>

The doing of activity is clear and without things.

<a id="u05074"></a>

Take the foremost of supreme accomplishments;

<a id="u05075"></a>

make the wheel of adornments at the six places. [N-220](../LEGACY-NOTES.md#n-220)

<a id="u05076"></a>

Furthermore, reliance and entry:

<a id="u05077"></a>

relying on the lama ceases the flow of samsara;

<a id="u05078"></a>

relying on empowerment ripens body and voice;

<a id="u05079"></a>

relying on pledges brings accomplishments;[^G-U05079]

<a id="a2000-c05-s01"></a>

*Scan-restored main verses:*[^G-A2000-C05-S01]

through reliance on the view, the three realms are cut off;<br />
through reliance on cultivation, the flow of delusion stops;

<a id="u05080"></a>

relying on activity trains the body.

<a id="u05081"></a>

Whatever arises from these objects of reliance

<a id="u05082"></a>

is itself pledges and vows.

<a id="u05083"></a>

Activities of entry have eight aspects;

<a id="u05084"></a>

bodily entry is fivefold;

<a id="u05085"></a>

voice has eight aspects;

<a id="u05086"></a>

ordinary mind's entry is thirty-twofold.

<a id="u05087"></a>

The special functioning of body and ordinary mind

<a id="u05088"></a>

is the distinction between karma and primordial knowing. [N-220](../LEGACY-NOTES.md#n-220)

<a id="u05089"></a>

Furthermore, the abiding-wheel of cultivation:

<a id="u05090"></a>

through training the body with diverse bodily exercises,

<a id="u05091"></a>

ordinary mind comes to abide.

<a id="u05092"></a>

Through three applications, soothe the channels;

<a id="u05093"></a>

draw ordinary mind in gently.

<a id="u05094"></a>

Through applications of signs and skillful craft—[^G-U05094]

<a id="u05095"></a>

through the distinct methods for body and ordinary mind,

<a id="u05096"></a>

make the yogin's ordinary mind abide. [N-220](../LEGACY-NOTES.md#n-220)

<a id="u05097"></a>

Furthermore, cut the flow of deluded clinging.

<a id="u05098"></a>

Gather the light of sun and moon; draw in the ocean.

<a id="u05099"></a>

Weigh the Mount Merus of a three-thousandfold world on a balance.

<a id="u05100"></a>

Reverse the Ground of ignorance: self-awareness. [N-221](../LEGACY-NOTES.md#n-221)

<a id="u05101"></a>

Draw samsara out entirely; block the path of passing beyond sorrow.

<a id="u05102"></a>

Sweep away phenomena and persons.

<a id="u05103"></a>

The result is self-complete, the Ground of the nature of phenomena;

<a id="u05104"></a>

doing and acting self-cease.

<a id="u05105"></a>

Draw out from the root craving that desires another.

<a id="u05106"></a>

Delusion concerning phenomena makes an upper basis (yas gzhi). [N-221](../LEGACY-NOTES.md#n-221)[^G-U05106]

<a id="u05107"></a>

That itself is the object liberated in its own place.

<a id="u05108"></a>

The three realms made the path, there is no buddha.

<a id="u05109"></a>

For whom could the teacher's teaching exist? It does not.

<a id="u05110"></a>

All phenomena are without a Ground; for whom could a root exist?

<a id="u05111"></a>

Ordinary mind free from existence and without desire,

<a id="u05112"></a>

uncontrived in intrinsic nature, is beyond expression.

<a id="u05113"></a>

Deluded conceptual thought of doing and acting is exhausted.

<a id="u05114"></a>

There are no phenomena, no buddhas, and no sentient beings.

<a id="u05115"></a>

There is no I, no self, no apprehended object and no apprehending subject. [N-221](../LEGACY-NOTES.md#n-221)

<a id="u05116"></a>

Furthermore, I shall explain the one taste of the dharma embodiment.

<a id="u05117"></a>

It is not expressed in phrases;

<a id="u05118"></a>

there is not even mere abiding called ordinary mind.

<a id="u05119"></a>

Because it is absent, in the appearance of emptiness

<a id="u05120"></a>

the source of mindfulness and knowing is exhausted.

<a id="u05121"></a>

In the nature of phenomena in which nothing is done,

<a id="u05122"></a>

there is no arising. Such great wonder!

<a id="u05123"></a>

In the dharma embodiment without arising,

<a id="u05124"></a>

there is no going. Such great wonder!

<a id="u05125"></a>

In awareness with nowhere to circle,

<a id="u05126"></a>

there is the nature of phenomena without arising.

<a id="u05127"></a>

Furthermore, I shall explain gathering and evenness.

<a id="u05128"></a>

Rejecting multiplicity, one is free from convention.

<a id="u05129"></a>

One is not established; intrinsic nature is pure.

<a id="u05130"></a>

Intrinsic nature being clear, meaning is accomplished.

<a id="u05131"></a>

Self-purity and the foundation,

<a id="u05132"></a>

and the foundation mixed with samsara—[^G-U05132]

<a id="u05133"></a>

hollow and without a core:

<a id="u05134"></a>

called ‘ya ma rla,’ beyond indication. [N-222](../LEGACY-NOTES.md#n-222)

<a id="u05135"></a>

Furthermore, I shall explain the delusion of intrinsic nature.

<a id="u05136"></a>

Time and faculties,

<a id="u05137"></a>

distinctions, entry, and respective activities;

<a id="u05138"></a>

aggregates, elements, and sense bases—

<a id="u05139"></a>

in an instant when causes and conditions coincide,

<a id="u05140"></a>

like jewels and masses of cloud,

<a id="u05141"></a>

they circle like a body and its shadow.

<a id="u05142"></a>

Through different dependencies upon the basis of delusion,

<a id="u05143"></a>

the activities of sentient beings are diverse. [N-222](../LEGACY-NOTES.md#n-222)

<a id="u05144"></a>

Furthermore, I shall explain pure appearance.

<a id="u05145"></a>

The vision of the direct perception of the nature of phenomena

<a id="u05146"></a>

definitely emerges through the faculties' gates

<a id="u05147"></a>

and is clear in cloudless space.

<a id="u05148"></a>

The vision of increasing experience:

<a id="u05149"></a>

the colors of primordial knowing come forth,

<a id="u05150"></a>

standing upright and arising as spokes,

<a id="u05151"></a>

as diverse spheres and embodiments,

<a id="u05152"></a>

clear in the domain of coarse appearance.

<a id="u05153"></a>

The vision of awareness reaching its limit:

<a id="u05154"></a>

the complete enjoyment embodiment's marks and signs are clear;

<a id="u05155"></a>

from rainbows of indeterminate colors,

<a id="u05156"></a>

the five families appear as fathers and mothers.

<a id="u05157"></a>

The vision of the exhaustion of nature of phenomena:

<a id="u05158"></a>

appearances of experience are emptied out,

<a id="u05159"></a>

the body is exhausted, and the objects of the faculties too are exhausted;

<a id="u05160"></a>

the assemblage of realizations and delusion is self-liberated, [N-223](../LEGACY-NOTES.md#n-223)

<a id="u05161"></a>

and there is freedom from expressed phrases.

<a id="u05162"></a>

Furthermore, I shall explain the sounds and signs of the elements.

<a id="u05163"></a>

When fire's sound crackles,

<a id="u05164"></a>

and sparks wave and proliferate,

<a id="u05165"></a>

the yogin does not accomplish the aim.

<a id="u05166"></a>

When it rises openly, blazing without interruption,

<a id="u05167"></a>

slow and long, with a sharp tip,

<a id="u05168"></a>

the yogin accomplishes a great aim. [N-224](../LEGACY-NOTES.md#n-224)

<a id="u05169"></a>

I shall also explain the colors.

<a id="u05170"></a>

When it is red with a black tip,

<a id="u05171"></a>

and pieces break off toward oneself,

<a id="u05172"></a>

obstacles occur.

<a id="u05173"></a>

White and clear, like a lotus;

<a id="u05174"></a>

sharp and of diverse colors;

<a id="u05175"></a>

wheels, parasols, weapon-points;

<a id="u05176"></a>

red, clear, limpid rays proliferating;

<a id="u05177"></a>

vajras, victory banners, bells,

<a id="u05178"></a>

checkerboard patterns, tents and houses,

<a id="u05179"></a>

tiered stupas, and so forth:

<a id="u05180"></a>

for the yogin, there is great completeness. [N-224](../LEGACY-NOTES.md#n-224)

<a id="u05181"></a>

Having directly perceived the meaning,

<a id="u05182"></a>

one passes beyond the three realms.

<a id="u05183"></a>

Furthermore, the subsidiary aspects of the elements:

<a id="u05184"></a>

water resounds with diverse sounds

<a id="u05185"></a>

and shows many white designs;

<a id="u05186"></a>

the yogin excellently accomplishes the aim.

<a id="u05187"></a>

Earth, through bouncing and ringing,

<a id="u05188"></a>

leaping at the edges and rolling, brings accomplishment.

<a id="u05189"></a>

Wind, through whistling and pressing,

<a id="u05190"></a>

and through being blue-green and billowing, does likewise. [N-224](../LEGACY-NOTES.md#n-224)

<a id="u05191"></a>

If one has not met with such signs,

<a id="u05192"></a>

the adept repeats the rite and undertakes it again.

<a id="u05193"></a>

Through three, five, or seven times,

<a id="u05194"></a>

it will be accomplished; there is no doubt about this.

<a id="u05195"></a>

Accord definitely with the respective signs. [N-224](../LEGACY-NOTES.md#n-224)

<a id="u05196"></a>

Thus, from the Great All-Penetrating Word, Root of All Phenomena:

<a id="u05197"></a>

the fifth chapter, the Array of View and Cultivation, teaching the root of abiding.[^G-U05197]
