## Chapter 6

<a id="u05198"></a>

Then, although all phenomena are inexpressible,

<a id="u05199"></a>

from the many deluded conceptual thoughts of sentient beings,

<a id="u05200"></a>

also as a foundation for words, phrases, and conventions,

<a id="u05201"></a>

from uncontrived space,

<a id="u05202"></a>

phrases arise: the teacher abides in distinction. [N-225](../LEGACY-NOTES.md#n-225)

<a id="u05203"></a>

To the buddha arisen from the lotus,

<a id="u05204"></a>

Maker of Joy supplicated thus:

<a id="u05205"></a>

‘Kye kye! You who abide in the lotus's center,

<a id="u05206"></a>

here in the mandala of space,

<a id="u05207"></a>

sixty-four groups of a hundred thousand

<a id="u05208"></a>

drops of light appear.

<a id="u05209"></a>

What is the cause of the seventeen utmost refinements?

<a id="u05210"></a>

What is the dharma embodiment like?

<a id="u05211"></a>

To what is complete enjoyment embodiment applied?

<a id="u05212"></a>

How many emanation embodiments are there?

<a id="u05213"></a>

What are the characteristics of embodiment?

<a id="u05214"></a>

How does it abide?

<a id="u05215"></a>

What are the embodiments of intrinsic nature and the nature of phenomena?

<a id="u05216"></a>

Blessed one, speak to me.’

<a id="u05217"></a>

Then the blessed Vajradhara

<a id="u05218"></a>

rose from the lotus's center

<a id="u05219"></a>

and made the gesture of the sky-soarer.

<a id="u05220"></a>

Merely by doing this,

<a id="u05221"></a>

from the space of the nature of phenomena's self-appearance,

<a id="u05222"></a>

from inexpressible intrinsic nature,

<a id="u05223"></a>

as expressed phrases, these words

<a id="u05224"></a>

arose naturally, without being made. [N-225](../LEGACY-NOTES.md#n-225)

<a id="u05225"></a>

**[Source heading: First reply.]**

<a id="u05226"></a>

‘Kye kye! Lord of Gods, listen.

<a id="u05227"></a>

I shall truly explain the sixty-four

<a id="u05228"></a>

groups of a hundred thousand white lights.

<a id="u05229"></a>

From the Great Perfection of intrinsic nature,

<a id="u05230"></a>

distinctions of phrases proliferate;

<a id="u05231"></a>

from three divisions, there are nine expanses.

<a id="u05232"></a>

When phrases are gathered, there are ślokas:

<a id="u05233"></a>

sixty-four groups of a hundred thousand,

<a id="u05234"></a>

with thirty-five thousand chapters

<a id="u05235"></a>

and twenty-one thousand textual sections.

<a id="u05236"></a>

What is expressed equals the bounds of space

<a id="u05237"></a>

and cuts the conceptual thoughts of beings to be trained. [N-226](../LEGACY-NOTES.md#n-226)

<a id="u05238"></a>

Here, when the subject matter is closely condensed,

<a id="u05239"></a>

there are a hundred and eighty fixings,

<a id="u05240"></a>

a thousand and a half crucial junctures,

<a id="u05241"></a>

three thousand gatherings of great crucial points,

<a id="u05242"></a>

four hundred thousand deviations and obscurations,

<a id="u05243"></a>

and twenty thousand named continua.

<a id="u05244"></a>

The sequences of expressed phrases are clearly arrayed. [N-226](../LEGACY-NOTES.md#n-226)

<a id="u05245"></a>

**[Source heading: Second reply.]**

<a id="u05246"></a>

I shall explain the seventeen utmost refinements of light:

<a id="u05247"></a>

Self-Arising and Self-Liberation,

<a id="u05248"></a>

Naturally Arisen Itself and Complete at the Root,[^G-U05248]

<a id="u05249"></a>

Endowed with Beauty and Jeweled Array,

<a id="u05250"></a>

Garland of Pearls and Without Letters— [N-227](../LEGACY-NOTES.md#n-227)

<a id="u05251"></a>

Mirror of the Core and Mirror of Awakened Mind,

<a id="u05252"></a>

Union of Sun and Moon, Adorned with Pointing Out,

<a id="u05253"></a>

Blazing Embodiment-Relics, Heap of Jewels,

<a id="u05254"></a>

Blazing Lamp, and Six Great Expanses.

<a id="u05255"></a>

Thus these sixteen sequences

<a id="u05256"></a>

definitely proliferate from Thal Gyur

<a id="u05257"></a>

and appear to sentient beings to be trained.

<a id="u05258"></a>

By holding the teaching in which vital content is gathered,

<a id="u05259"></a>

one liberates from samsara for as long as ages exist,

<a id="u05260"></a>

and complete buddhahood manifests. [N-228](../LEGACY-NOTES.md#n-228)

<a id="u05261"></a>

The dharma embodiment: speech and awakened mind,

<a id="u05262"></a>

qualities, and enlightened activity.

<a id="u05263"></a>

Embodiment is emptiness and clarity,

<a id="u05264"></a>

an intrinsic nature without signs.

<a id="u05265"></a>

Speech is free from words, phrases, and names,

<a id="u05266"></a>

free from expression and discussion.

<a id="u05267"></a>

Awakened mind, without thinking or movement,

<a id="u05268"></a>

is beyond proliferation and activity.

<a id="u05269"></a>

Its quality is neither coming back nor returning;

<a id="u05270"></a>

basic space and primordial knowing pervade,

<a id="u05271"></a>

and intrinsic nature is uncontrived.

<a id="u05272"></a>

Enlightened activity has not come about, arisen, or been done;

<a id="u05273"></a>

it comes about of its own force and does not cease.

<a id="u05274"></a>

Not done, there is no doing. [N-229](../LEGACY-NOTES.md#n-229)

<a id="u05275"></a>

**[Source heading: Fourth reply.]**

<a id="u05276"></a>

The complete enjoyment embodiment too has embodiment, speech, awakened mind,

<a id="u05277"></a>

qualities, and enlightened activity—five.

<a id="u05278"></a>

Embodiment appears but has no intrinsic nature.

<a id="u05279"></a>

Clarity and that which makes clear are pervaded by awareness.

<a id="u05280"></a>

Speech is naturally arisen and self-appearance,

<a id="u05281"></a>

a wheel of the aspects of proliferating and gathering.

<a id="u05282"></a>

Awakened mind is seeing without a flow,

<a id="u05283"></a>

the expressiveness of knowing, and enlightened intent. [N-229](../LEGACY-NOTES.md#n-229)

<a id="u05284"></a>

Qualities: the marks and signs are complete;

<a id="u05285"></a>

clear enlightened intent and higher knowing are complete.

<a id="u05286"></a>

Enlightened activity: from the wheel of Dharma,

<a id="u05287"></a>

outer, inner, and secret [teachings] unfold,

<a id="u05288"></a>

and for the buddhas of the respective families

<a id="u05289"></a>

and for bodhisattvas too,

<a id="u05290"></a>

they unfold from the naturally arisen tongue,

<a id="u05291"></a>

showing the retinue through their own essence. [N-229](../LEGACY-NOTES.md#n-229)

<a id="u05292"></a>

**[Source heading: Fifth reply.]**

<a id="u05293"></a>

The emanation embodiment too has embodiment, speech, awakened mind,

<a id="u05294"></a>

qualities, and enlightened activity—five in each case.

<a id="u05295"></a>

Embodiment, with marks and signs,

<a id="u05296"></a>

emanates bodies for whatever beings to be trained appear.

<a id="u05297"></a>

Speech has excellent phrase-composition:

<a id="u05298"></a>

the six aspects of Brahmā's voice.

<a id="u05299"></a>

Awakened mind, through primordial knowing of objects of knowledge,

<a id="u05300"></a>

abides in enlightened intent for the two benefits, one's own and others'.

<a id="u05301"></a>

Qualities are completeness of knowing.

<a id="u05302"></a>

Enlightened activity completes without remainder

<a id="u05303"></a>

the outer, inner, and secret deeds,

<a id="u05304"></a>

abiding in five perfections. [N-230](../LEGACY-NOTES.md#n-230)

<a id="u05305"></a>

**[Source heading: Sixth reply.]**

<a id="u05306"></a>

Embodiment's characteristics, essence,

<a id="u05307"></a>

definitive wording, and divisions;

<a id="u05308"></a>

the respective fields and beings to be trained;

<a id="u05309"></a>

the activities of differentiation and the measure of arising—[^G-U05309]

<a id="u05310"></a>

through distinctions in characteristics and understanding:

<a id="u05311"></a>

characteristics are established from phenomena,

<a id="u05312"></a>

established from desirable qualities,

<a id="u05313"></a>

and established from diverse methods.

<a id="u05314"></a>

Understanding: intrinsic nature is pure;

<a id="u05315"></a>

the respective domains of appearance are shown;

<a id="u05316"></a>

and, endowed with form, the faculties are complete. [N-230](../LEGACY-NOTES.md#n-230)

<a id="u05317"></a>

**[Source heading: Seventh reply.]**

<a id="u05318"></a>

Embodiment's manner of abiding has three aspects:

<a id="u05319"></a>

because it is liberated from phenomena of signs,

<a id="u05320"></a>

it is not a form marked by divisions;

<a id="u05321"></a>

because the intrinsic nature of clarity is complete,

<a id="u05322"></a>

it appears but has no intrinsic nature;

<a id="u05323"></a>

to train the continua of those to be trained,

<a id="u05324"></a>

it abides unmoving in great equipoise.

<a id="u05325"></a>

Unmoving and clear,

<a id="u05326"></a>

it appears as the aspect of awareness and knowing:

<a id="u05327"></a>

even, without partiality, and undivided. [N-230](../LEGACY-NOTES.md#n-230)

<a id="u05328"></a>

**[Source heading: Eighth reply.]**

<a id="u05329"></a>

The intrinsic nature and nature of phenomena of embodiment:

<a id="u05330"></a>

intrinsic nature is emptiness and clarity,

<a id="u05331"></a>

the unceasing manifestation of knowing and awareness.

<a id="u05332"></a>

The nature of phenomena of embodiment is complete;

<a id="u05333"></a>

there is no return, going, or coming,

<a id="u05334"></a>

and no speaking, thinking, or expression.

<a id="u05335"></a>

Furthermore, accomplishing embodiments:

<a id="u05336"></a>

through the distinctions taught above,

<a id="u05337"></a>

in one's own ordinary mind, the seed of the dharma embodiment,

<a id="u05338"></a>

apply the crucial point of direct perception,

<a id="u05339"></a>

and become constantly familiar with it.

<a id="u05340"></a>

This is accomplishing the dharma embodiment.

<a id="u05341"></a>

Train in desirable qualities, the seed of the complete enjoyment embodiment,

<a id="u05342"></a>

and become familiar with them:

<a id="u05343"></a>

this is accomplishing complete enjoyment.

<a id="u05344"></a>

As for the seed of the emanation embodiment, the four elements:

<a id="u05345"></a>

train in their word and meaning;

<a id="u05346"></a>

through familiarity, the emanation embodiment is accomplished. [N-231](../LEGACY-NOTES.md#n-231)

<a id="u05347"></a>

Furthermore, I shall explain the scope of fire's activities.

<a id="u05348"></a>

Distinguished through colors:

<a id="u05349"></a>

white, clear, and gentle accomplishes pacifying;

<a id="u05350"></a>

yellow, subtle, and long accomplishes increasing;

<a id="u05351"></a>

red, limpid, and proliferating is the activity of power;

<a id="u05352"></a>

blue-green, black, and short accomplishes the fierce.

<a id="u05353"></a>

When it is variegated, broken, and whirling,

<a id="u05354"></a>

distinctions of various activities are accomplished.

<a id="u05355"></a>

When it is extremely subtle, there is falling.

<a id="u05356"></a>

When broken and fierce, obstacles arise.

<a id="u05357"></a>

When spreading tongues of fire have smoke,

<a id="u05358"></a>

pledges deteriorate. [N-232](../LEGACY-NOTES.md#n-232)

<a id="u05359"></a>

Distinctions through sound:

<a id="u05360"></a>

three successive lhab lhab sounds and many spar spar sounds

<a id="u05361"></a>

accomplish diverse [activities].

<a id="u05362"></a>

Fierceness and crackling [accomplish] the fierce;

<a id="u05363"></a>

roaring and dense [sound accomplish] pacifying and increasing, the two;

<a id="u05364"></a>

resounding and ringing accomplish the supreme. [N-232](../LEGACY-NOTES.md#n-232)

<a id="u05365"></a>

Furthermore, I shall explain the scope of water's activities.

<a id="u05366"></a>

The definitive signs of colors:

<a id="u05367"></a>

through the four beginning with white,

<a id="u05368"></a>

pacifying and the rest are definitely accomplished.

<a id="u05369"></a>

When brown-black, rough, and whirling,

<a id="u05370"></a>

the yogin does not accomplish the aim.

<a id="u05371"></a>

When white and clear, rising and billowing,

<a id="u05372"></a>

the supreme accomplishment is definitely attained.

<a id="u05373"></a>

Through sounds that resound and ring,

<a id="u05374"></a>

whoosh, roar, and are dense,

<a id="u05375"></a>

food, clothing, and the result are accomplished.

<a id="u05376"></a>

Through sir, sil, and lhab sounds,

<a id="u05377"></a>

the bliss of each of the three embodiments is attained. [N-232](../LEGACY-NOTES.md#n-232)

<a id="u05378"></a>

Furthermore, I shall explain the scope of earth's activities.

<a id="u05379"></a>

Know white, red, and the other colors from the preceding account.

<a id="u05380"></a>

Indeterminate change does not accomplish the aim.

<a id="u05381"></a>

Clarity and shining accomplish the supreme.

<a id="u05382"></a>

Its sounds are shag and sdig— [N-232](../LEGACY-NOTES.md#n-232)

<a id="a2000-c06-s01"></a>

*Scan-restored main verses:*[^G-A2000-C06-S01]

through tog and sing, various activities [are accomplished];<br />
through roaring and sing, the supreme is attained.

<a id="u05383"></a>

Furthermore, I shall explain the scope of wind's activities.

<a id="u05384"></a>

White, red, and the other colors are as above.

<a id="u05385"></a>

Blackness and turbulence do not accomplish the aim.

<a id="u05386"></a>

Variegated blazing accomplishes the supreme.

<a id="u05387"></a>

Its sounds are roaring, muffled, and diverse;

<a id="u05388"></a>

ringing and diverse [sounds] bring supreme attainment. [N-232](../LEGACY-NOTES.md#n-232)

<a id="u05389"></a>

Furthermore, I shall explain signs and intrinsic nature.

<a id="u05390"></a>

After the preceding signs arise,

<a id="u05391"></a>

after three months and six months,

<a id="u05392"></a>

through the two, one knows the sounds of the insentient and the aware.

<a id="u05393"></a>

After ten months and eighteen months,

<a id="u05394"></a>

each respective sound and the voices of the six classes

<a id="u05395"></a>

are known just as they are uttered. [N-233](../LEGACY-NOTES.md#n-233)

<a id="u05396"></a>

Furthermore, the stages of the result:

<a id="u05397"></a>

the dharma embodiment is through the activity of ordinary mind;

<a id="u05398"></a>

through complete enjoyment embodiment, [it] is attained;[^G-U05398]

<a id="u05399"></a>

the emanation embodiment is through the body.

<a id="u05400"></a>

Essence itself is through equal portions. [N-233](../LEGACY-NOTES.md#n-233)

<a id="u05401"></a>

The body: the elements are pure.

<a id="u05402"></a>

Voice is joined to āli and kāli.

<a id="u05403"></a>

Ordinary mind, without things, is unimpeded penetration.

<a id="u05404"></a>

The gathering of the three is great bliss.

<a id="u05405"></a>

Furthermore, this is to be fully explained:

<a id="u05406"></a>

through thoroughly knowing embodiment's characteristics,

<a id="u05407"></a>

all delusion ceases at one time.

<a id="u05408"></a>

Through fully knowing embodiment's intrinsic nature,

<a id="u05409"></a>

the boundary between clarity and emptiness is held.

<a id="u05410"></a>

Through knowing essence, there is purification into emptiness.

<a id="u05411"></a>

Through knowing ‘dang’ [reading unresolved], non-conceptuality arises. [N-234](../LEGACY-NOTES.md#n-234)[^G-U05411]

<a id="u05412"></a>

Knowing the great identity,

<a id="u05413"></a>

one cuts the root of samsara in the three realms.

<a id="u05414"></a>

Through these, confidence in self-liberation

<a id="u05415"></a>

is certainly attained without anything being done.

<a id="u05416"></a>

Ema! Such wondrous marvel!

<a id="u05417"></a>

In space in which nothing is done,

<a id="u05418"></a>

uncontrived phrases arise;

<a id="u05419"></a>

unthought, naturally arisen, they are understood by the conceptual mind.

<a id="u05420"></a>

Having understood thus, through knowing and realization,

<a id="u05421"></a>

[one trusts] that, as the root of all the scriptural collections,

<a id="u05422"></a>

this king of continua, Thal Gyur itself,

<a id="u05423"></a>

this most secret, unsurpassed teaching,

<a id="u05424"></a>

is indeed this. [N-235](../LEGACY-NOTES.md#n-235)

<a id="u05425"></a>

All the retinues appearing through intrinsic nature,

<a id="u05426"></a>

having aroused an utterly delighted ordinary mind,

<a id="u05427"></a>

read and preserve this continuum,

<a id="u05428"></a>

write it and hold it;

<a id="u05429"></a>

holding and preserving it at the throat and crown,

<a id="u05430"></a>

they engage in honoring all buddhas.

<a id="u05431"></a>

Having accepted and pledged this,

<a id="u05432"></a>

they praise the teacher, the blessed buddha—

<a id="u05433"></a>

his embodiment complete in qualities. [N-235](../LEGACY-NOTES.md#n-235)

<a id="u05434"></a>

Then all those fully assembled,

<a id="u05435"></a>

with ordinary minds of joy, delight, and gladness arisen,

<a id="u05436"></a>

[behold] space, self-purified and clear,

<a id="u05437"></a>

and inexpressible phrases self-arising.

<a id="u05438"></a>

Rejoicing and finding bliss,

<a id="u05439"></a>

they become even with freedom from conceptual elaborations

<a id="u05440"></a>

in the Great Perfection of intrinsic nature. [N-235](../LEGACY-NOTES.md#n-235)

<a id="u05441"></a>

Of all phenomena as the single unique sphere,

<a id="u05442"></a>

having attained the enlightened intent of equal taste,

<a id="u05443"></a>

[to] the lotus-arisen blessed one,

<a id="u05444"></a>

they say, ‘Excellent! Excellent! Excellent!

<a id="u05445"></a>

You have ripened us as the three embodiments;

<a id="u05446"></a>

we supplicate and rejoice.’ [N-235](../LEGACY-NOTES.md#n-235)

<a id="u05447"></a>

This is called the Array of Embodiments,

<a id="u05448"></a>

the sixth chapter.[^G-U05448]
