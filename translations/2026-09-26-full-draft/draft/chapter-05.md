[U04795](../alignment/chapter-05.md#u04795) have excellent faith and diligence,

[U04798](../alignment/chapter-05.md#u04798) unchanging and utterly stable, they hold it.  
[U04799](../alignment/chapter-05.md#u04799) Alternatively, one whose own impetus is quiet,

[U04807](../alignment/chapter-05.md#u04807) spontaneous presence from the side of diversity;  
[U04808](../alignment/chapter-05.md#u04808) indeterminacy from the aspect of the extent of change;

[U04810](../alignment/chapter-05.md#u04810) appearance's expressiveness transforming into anything;  
[U04811](../alignment/chapter-05.md#u04811) all arising, together with affirmation;  
[U04812](../alignment/chapter-05.md#u04812) primordial purity because delusion is purified;  
[U04813](../alignment/chapter-05.md#u04813) and the variegation held to be diverse manners of appearance. [N-206](../REVIEW-NOTES.md#n-206)  
[U04814](../alignment/chapter-05.md#u04814) Through such distinctions in what is asserted,  
[U04815](../alignment/chapter-05.md#u04815) because the Great Perfection appears as anything,  
[U04816](../alignment/chapter-05.md#u04816) although differing assertions are numerous,  
[U04817](../alignment/chapter-05.md#u04817) in the view of abiding in its own disposition  
[U04818](../alignment/chapter-05.md#u04818) there is no one, no two, and freedom from anything.

[U04822](../alignment/chapter-05.md#u04822) are beyond the extremes of existence, nonexistence, and emptiness.  
[U04823](../alignment/chapter-05.md#u04823) In brief, they are distinguished through characteristics.

[U04832](../alignment/chapter-05.md#u04832) nothing done and nothing arisen;  
[U04833](../alignment/chapter-05.md#u04833) unaltered appearance, self-purified;

[U04836](../alignment/chapter-05.md#u04836) not through discerning knowing,

[U04842](../alignment/chapter-05.md#u04842) Because seeing the nature of phenomena purifies,  
[U04843](../alignment/chapter-05.md#u04843) there is no good or bad in activities.

[U04845](../alignment/chapter-05.md#u04845) virtue and wrongdoing, both, are removed. [N-207](../REVIEW-NOTES.md#n-207)  
[U04846](../alignment/chapter-05.md#u04846) Without seeing self-awareness directly,  
[U04847](../alignment/chapter-05.md#u04847) the assertions of the nine stages do not liberate.  
[U04848](../alignment/chapter-05.md#u04848) When the nature of phenomena is exhausted and its limit reached,

[U04885](../alignment/chapter-05.md#u04885) [Source heading: Eighth reply.]  
[U04886](../alignment/chapter-05.md#u04886) What is called cultivation is ordinary mind abiding,  
[U04887](../alignment/chapter-05.md#u04887) cutting outer and inner movement,  
[U04888](../alignment/chapter-05.md#u04888) and ceasing apprehended object and apprehending subject.  
[U04889](../alignment/chapter-05.md#u04889) What arises from intrinsic nature's own disposition

[U04892](../alignment/chapter-05.md#u04892) the body's crucial points, the eyes, and emptiness—  
[U04893](../alignment/chapter-05.md#u04893) these are held to be cultivation.  
[U04894](../alignment/chapter-05.md#u04894) The light and colors of letters,  
[U04895](../alignment/chapter-05.md#u04895) and the emanations' proliferating and gathering,  
[U04896](../alignment/chapter-05.md#u04896) are explained as deep absorption.  
[U04897](../alignment/chapter-05.md#u04897) Uncontrived, resting of itself, is the natural state.  
[U04898](../alignment/chapter-05.md#u04898) Because there is no change from that, however [it occurs],  
[U04899](../alignment/chapter-05.md#u04899) it is the buddhas' enlightened intent. [N-210](../REVIEW-NOTES.md#n-210)
