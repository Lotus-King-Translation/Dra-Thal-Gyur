[U04344](../alignment/chapter-04.md#u04344) [Source heading: First subject of inquiry.]  
[U04345](../alignment/chapter-04.md#u04345) The nature of phenomena of abiding is as follows:  
[U04346](../alignment/chapter-04.md#u04346) abiding in its fundamental disposition, it is free from virtue and wrongdoing;  
[U04347](../alignment/chapter-04.md#u04347) abiding as cause and result, it is free from dependence;  
[U04348](../alignment/chapter-04.md#u04348) abiding through conditions, the elements are pure—  
[U04349](../alignment/chapter-04.md#u04349) it does not shift or change. [N-187](../REVIEW-NOTES.md#n-187)  

[U04350](../alignment/chapter-04.md#u04350) [Source heading: Second subject of inquiry.]  
[U04351](../alignment/chapter-04.md#u04351) The nature of phenomena of the intervals of movement is like this:  
[U04352](../alignment/chapter-04.md#u04352) I shall explain it fully as a great wonder.  
[U04353](../alignment/chapter-04.md#u04353) Wind, according to the sequence of a day,  
[U04354](../alignment/chapter-04.md#u04354) is reckoned as twenty thousand, one thousand,  
[U04355](../alignment/chapter-04.md#u04355) and six hundred;  
[U04356](../alignment/chapter-04.md#u04356) the winds of one day are thereby complete.  
[U04357](../alignment/chapter-04.md#u04357) According to the particular distinctions,  
[U04358](../alignment/chapter-04.md#u04358) for the young there are sixty extra;  
[U04359](../alignment/chapter-04.md#u04359) for the old there is a shortfall.  
[U04360](../alignment/chapter-04.md#u04360) This is through the activity of the elemental vessel. [N-188](../REVIEW-NOTES.md#n-188)  
[U04361](../alignment/chapter-04.md#u04361) Among sixty movements of wind,  
[U04362](../alignment/chapter-04.md#u04362) the subtle movement of the nature of phenomena is held through reckoning.  
[U04363](../alignment/chapter-04.md#u04363) From two such groups it is coarse;  
[U04364](../alignment/chapter-04.md#u04364) when these are gathered as three, it is gross.  
[U04365](../alignment/chapter-04.md#u04365) Subtract three from that: it is even. [N-188](../REVIEW-NOTES.md#n-188)  
[U04366](../alignment/chapter-04.md#u04366) In brief, within twenty movements,  
[U04367](../alignment/chapter-04.md#u04367) there is one evenness of the nature of phenomena.  

[U04368](../alignment/chapter-04.md#u04368) [Source heading: Third subject of inquiry.]  
[U04369](../alignment/chapter-04.md#u04369) The nature of phenomena of time is as follows:  
[U04370](../alignment/chapter-04.md#u04370) outer, inner, secret,  
[U04371](../alignment/chapter-04.md#u04371) and suchness—four.  
[U04372](../alignment/chapter-04.md#u04372) The outer is the second [month] of autumn,  
[U04373](../alignment/chapter-04.md#u04373) showing the abiding of the nature of phenomena of time.  
[U04374](../alignment/chapter-04.md#u04374) The inner is the time when dawn breaks.  
[U04375](../alignment/chapter-04.md#u04375) The secret is sleep and dreams.  
[U04376](../alignment/chapter-04.md#u04376) Suchness is held through wind.  
[U04377](../alignment/chapter-04.md#u04377) When the time is reached, it is complete. [N-188](../REVIEW-NOTES.md#n-188)  

[U04378](../alignment/chapter-04.md#u04378) [Source heading: Fourth subject of inquiry.]  
[U04379](../alignment/chapter-04.md#u04379) The nature of phenomena of buddhas:  
[U04380](../alignment/chapter-04.md#u04380) undivided, not dividing, even—  
[U04381](../alignment/chapter-04.md#u04381) how could words, phrases, and names exist there?  
[U04382](../alignment/chapter-04.md#u04382) The basis of dependence is self-purified, and delusion is purified.  
[U04383](../alignment/chapter-04.md#u04383) It is not known by the names samsara and nirvana.  
[U04384](../alignment/chapter-04.md#u04384) Not having come about or arisen, it does not cease.  
[U04385](../alignment/chapter-04.md#u04385) All-arising primordial knowing is not any [thing]. [N-187](../REVIEW-NOTES.md#n-187)  
[U04386](../alignment/chapter-04.md#u04386) There is no taking and no liberation.  
[U04387](../alignment/chapter-04.md#u04387) In a moment free from causes and conditions,  
[U04388](../alignment/chapter-04.md#u04388) buddhas and sentient beings appear as a foundation,  
[U04389](../alignment/chapter-04.md#u04389) free from all multiplicity and parts. [N-187](../REVIEW-NOTES.md#n-187)

[U04391](../alignment/chapter-04.md#u04391) As for deluded sentient beings:  
[U04392](../alignment/chapter-04.md#u04392) embodiments and primordial knowing are in their own manner,

[U04397](../alignment/chapter-04.md#u04397) without entering into surrounding conditions,  
[U04398](../alignment/chapter-04.md#u04398) in unobstructedness and penetration,  
[U04399](../alignment/chapter-04.md#u04399) from the appearance aspects of object and knowing,

[U04401](../alignment/chapter-04.md#u04401) The desirable qualities are self-complete.
