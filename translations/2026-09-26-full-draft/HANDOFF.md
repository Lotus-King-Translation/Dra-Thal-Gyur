# Handoff — full working draft

## Coverage

The saved English draft represents U00001–U05466 of the unchanged Adzom e-text: all six chapters and eighteen closing-material units. The continuation after checkpoint `9958c71` drafted U04001–U05466. There are no unprocessed e-text units. Represented does not mean that every phrase has a settled translation.

Two additional scan-only records are preserved. S0001 is a provisionally transcribed and translated opening caption. S0002, a short annotation at the chapter-one/two transition, remains untranscribed and untranslated; its explicit marker and image evidence are included rather than omitted.

## What was done

Drafting followed the active combined translation standard and established glossary, with source consultation, contextual reading, translator self-checks and source-linked notes. Main text, source variants, headings, ritual syllables and closing seals remain distinguishable. Local corrections are recorded separately from raw batches and applied during assembly. Some initial batch-indexing errors were corrected before assembly; the affected notes describe those repairs.

Bookkeeping is computed from saved files. The assembly checks test complete and unique unit coverage, nonempty English segments, exact source-slice reconstruction, note-marker definitions, unchanged source and guidance/glossary hashes, assembled-output round-trip equality, and the eight-column schema of terminology proposals. These checks do not measure semantic accuracy. The independent-QC regression fixtures were not run, and no independent QC clearance is claimed.

All 205 facsimile pages have been opened during accumulated source consultation. This does not constitute full diplomatic transcription or page-by-page collation. Exact unit IDs and Unicode-character offsets are the stable locators. Associated scan-page values in review notes have not been independently validated as an exact unit-to-page concordance.

## Open review work

The notes retain uncertain readings, number groupings, syntax, referents, proper names, title allusions and terminology gaps. Explicitly retained source expressions include `mar gar` at U04224, `gyi nar` at U04394, `lag bcangs` at U04996, `ya ma rla` at U05134 and `dang` at U05411. Ritual syllables are preserved without inventing a semantic translation. These examples are not an exhaustive list of unresolved matters.

Proposed terminology has not been activated in the canonical glossary. Declared usages and additional exact-form matches are separate records. The latter are review candidates, not approved applications: compounds, inflection, context and exception scope still require a construction-by-construction audit. Repeated use of provisional wording does not make it an approved default.

Difficult ethical paradoxes, ritual claims, bodily instructions and accounts of death are translated as textual material. The package does not endorse harmful acts or certify these passages as safe practical, medical or psychological guidance. Their local context and unresolved symbolic referents must be retained in review.

## Disposition

The complete e-text working draft is assembled for source-based human review. Unresolved readings, the unread scan-only annotation, a full usage audit and independent quality control remain. This is not a fully resolved translation, an authenticated critical edition or a publication-cleared text. The Tibetan source, original glossary, and governing guidance are unchanged.
