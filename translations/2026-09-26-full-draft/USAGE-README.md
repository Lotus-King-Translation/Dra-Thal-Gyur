# Usage and terminology records

`USAGE.csv` contains 353 declared source-linked usage records from the translator's saved notes. The English cell contains the final assembled segment, not an assertion that every word in it is a glossary proposal. Some declared units supply surrounding context.

`USAGE-CANDIDATES.csv` contains 1361 additional exact-EWTS source-form matches. These are review candidates, not approved exceptions or a claim of complete semantic review. Compound precedence, attached forms, word senses and the scope of each proposal require source interpretation. An exact match may be a component of a longer established expression. Non-matching grammatical forms may still require review.

`PROPOSED-GLOSSARY.csv` and `PROPOSED-USAGES.csv` retain the active glossary's eight-column schema. The former holds candidate forms not exactly matching an established headword; the latter preserves the original pair where a headword matches and records proposed supplementary uses separately. Both are unapproved working records. One Tibetan form is used per row. Blank English cells mean that no single candidate is settled; alternatives remain in the status field, not competing canonical defaults.

Candidate Tibetan headwords are reversible renderings of saved EWTS forms with a final tsheg. They are not emendations of the source and may not equal an inflected source occurrence. Exact source quotations remain in the alignment and review notes. No source spelling, canonical pair, schema or glossary order was changed.

Assembly does not extend a local proposal to every later occurrence. The additional candidate index is intentionally separate from declared usage records; a full construction-by-construction usage audit remains necessary.
