# Chapter 5 — inherited locator notes

These are summaries of historical translation consultation, not fresh scan findings. Original notes remain in translations/2026-09-26-full-draft/REVIEW-NOTES.md. Page suggestions are unverified until matched to native text.

## N-204 — Chapter transition / light simile / question syntax
The opening stacks several ablative constructions before phrases arise like light from the tongue. The query U04777 is grammatically closer to an instruction to distinguish than to a fully marked interrogative.
PAGES 179, 180.

## N-205 — Recipient descriptions / source spelling
The first recipient is diligent and disciplined; the alternative description includes glen pa, lhod and rtogs pa chung. The last explicitly has rtogs with final s, not rtog.
PAGES 180, 181.

## N-206 — Sevenfold grouping / assertions
The source announces seven ways of abiding, with compressed features involving diversity, change, certainty, expressiveness, affirmation, purity and variegation.
PAGES 181.

## N-207 — Ethical rhetoric / enumeration / instructional scope
The speaker includes taking life and taking what is not given among phenomena arising from me, and the view-of-phrases list includes taking life and freedom from virtue/wrongdoing. The text then contrasts conceptual assertions with direct perception and explicitly negates discerning knowing as the means here.
PAGES 181, 182.

## N-208 — View of phrases/meaning / negative transmission clause
The source distinguishes mental examination from direct engagement with visionary chains. It then says the essence of view is not shown through transmission or pith instructions, despite prescribing both elsewhere.
PAGES 182, 183.

## N-209 — Contextual chos can / four view categories
Here chos can is contrasted with chos nyid in the outer/inner views rather than used in the earlier formal proof construction. The list names unsurpassed, while its explanation uses suchness.
PAGES 183.

## N-210 — Distinct practice categories / compressed syntax
The passage explicitly distinguishes cultivation, meditative stabilization, deep absorption and the natural state. It describes letter-light emanations with spro bsdu rather than the exact 'phro 'du headword.
PAGES 183.

## N-211 — Negation scope / deviation list
The first deviations are explicitly negated, while the following descriptions culminate in the condition not falling to the side of any of these. Some phrases, including cha lugs and skyur byed, are compressed and difficult.
PAGES 184.

## N-212 — Source form / cultivation versus failure to cultivate
U04936 contains rigs pa, not rig pa. The subsequent section concerns faults of not having cultivated, not necessarily the separately established non-cultivation state.
PAGES 184, 185.

## N-213 — Rhetorical negations / ethical list without predicate
The pure-seeing account denies the need for several rituals and follows with an unpredicated list of taking life, taking what is not given and unchaste activity. The next clause denies view, cultivation and activity. The chapter later expressly requires offerings and pledges.
PAGES 185, 186.

## N-214 — Fourfold grouping / assertions of identity
The source gives desire, hatred, dull confusion and awakening with an explicit four, then relates conceptualization, karma, delusion and habitual tendencies to realization and the nature of phenomena.
PAGES 186.

## N-215 — Contrived-view passage / unresolved idiom
The passage is introduced as contrived view, gives a difficult sequence of ungenerated buddha/result/body statements, and then describes cutting the root of nirvana and ripening samsara. The final lag bcangs idiom is unresolved.
PAGES 186, 187.

## N-216 — Paradoxical instruction / continuation
The text says that without cutting life one does not become buddha, then gives an image of windows, darkness and later explicit reversals of ordinary practice language. The local passage alone does not settle what life or windows denote.
PAGES 187.

## N-217 — Symbolic reversals / syntax / ethical rhetoric
This continues U04998's paradoxical life-cutting statement. Dharma leads to lower destinies, cultivating gathers afflictions, and a person expounds scripture while taking another's life. The passage explicitly introduces symbolic meaning and closes its examples with ya re cha. The la construction at U05002 is difficult.
PAGES 187, 188.

## N-218 — Paradox / implied objects / numbers
The source juxtaposes uprooting samsara and nirvana, sharpness cutting nothing, spaciousness holding nothing, and all/one reversals involving killing and fetters. Several objects are omitted.
PAGES 188.

## N-219 — Conduct instructions / contrast with rhetorical negations
After paradoxical language, the source explicitly requires maintaining offerings, mantra, vows and pledges, and forbids disparaging women, companions, the lama and the aggregates. Desire and attachment are handled through successive instructions rather than a single simple prohibition.
PAGES 189, 190.

## N-220 — Practice sequence / ornaments / source variant / counts
Ointments, pills, sphere nets and ornaments are named without a complete practical specification. The text gives eight activities of entry, then five bodily, eight vocal and thirty-two mental entries. A small-print mtshan ma bzo yi variant is embedded in U05094.
PAGES 190, 191.

## N-221 — Cosmic imagery / source variant / negation
Gathering sun/moon light, drawing the ocean and weighing Meru precede a negative sequence about buddhas, phenomena and selves. U05106 embeds ye gzhi as a reported alternative beside yas gzhi in the e-text.
PAGES 191.

## N-222 — Source gloss / idiom / metaphor
The e-text embeds a reported foundation-of-reliance reading in a foundation-mixed-with-samsara clause. Ya ma rla is an unglossed idiom followed by beyond indication. Jewels/clouds and body/shadow then illustrate dependent differences.
PAGES 191, 192.

## N-223 — Four visions / source spelling
The named four-vision sequence uses shortened forms of established whole expressions. U05160 spells rtogs tshogs with final s, which differs from the conceptual-thought assemblage elsewhere.
PAGES 192, 193.

## N-224 — Elemental sounds and signs / onomatopoeia
Fire, water, earth and wind are described through sound-words, flame shapes and success/failure signs. Several onomatopoeic values are uncertain, including 'tsheg, stag/lhab, ldeg/sing and sbir/gzir.
PAGES 193, 194, 195.
