## Method

Continuous visual lexical proofreading of native-resolution, overlapping three-column crops against U-anchored e-text, using Tibetan and its EWTS aid. Main-text words are compared in full reading order across crop boundaries. Exact whitespace, tsheg/shad positioning and ornamental signs are not certified by this lexical pass. No scan-only comparison witnesses are covered. A page marked lexical_complete is not a declaration of complete diplomatic collation.

Read the six main-text lines of each page across three overlapping native-image crops, comparing their lexical sequence against the Tibetan e-text and its EWTS aid. Page boundaries below identify exactly where coverage starts/stops. Main-text agreement is a visual proofreading result, not a guarantee that no typographic detail remains.
