## Limits

- Complete lexical comparison is distinct from a complete diplomatic transcription: the e-text systematically doubles shad where the scan commonly shows single shad; individual punctuation is not certified by these lexical passes.
- Source small annotations are inventoried but secondary annotation audit is separate.
- PDF p60 U1522 remains uncertain at the fused glyph cluster པའི་རྒྱུ་; no conjectural correction proposed.
- PDF p101 U2615 supplied annotation not visually located; its Adzom scan provenance remains unresolved.
- Scope ends at U2631 on PDF p101. Chapter1 U2632–2635 and any p102 inscription lie outside this assigned page range.

The supplied e-text commonly has double shad where the scan shows a single shad. This pass does not certify each punctuation, line-fill, spacing, or typographic detail. It is a continuous lexical audit with explicit residual uncertainty, not a claim that Chapter 1 or the edition is fully completed.
