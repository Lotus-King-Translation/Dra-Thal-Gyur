# Langtang: inspection of the opening manifest gap

Scope: PDF pages 1–4 of `editions/langtang-manuscript/sgra-thal-gyur.pdf`, corresponding to manifest images 2, 3, 6, 7. Native embedded pixels were inspected, followed by unscaled detail crops of the boundary between images 3 and 6. This is a boundary check, not a full collation or complete foliation audit.

The manifest does not expose indices 4–5. **The inspected main text nevertheless appears continuous at this boundary.** The bottom of image 3 ends with `dbus su`; image 6 begins with `rlung sems ...`, continuing the phrase that corresponds to Adzom electronic unit U00017. The following text corresponds to the ensuing palace, uncontrived awareness, and purification verses. There is no observed two-page jump in the main-text sequence at this particular transition.

Decision: do not print a textual lacuna for missing image indices 4–5 on numerical evidence alone. Retain the manifest fact and record this observed continuity. This does not establish why the indices are absent, certify all intervening glyphs against Adzom, or resolve the later gaps at indices 38–41 and 172–173.
